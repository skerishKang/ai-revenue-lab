## hwpx-mcp (@kimdayoun/hwpx-mcp)

The council apps' HWPX export (apps/padiem-council-apps/static/document-export.js)
adapts the empty OWPML package skeleton (mimetype, version.xml, META-INF parts,
settings.xml, Contents/content.hpf, Contents/header.xml, Contents/section0.xml)
from the hwpx-mcp project. HWPX is a zip+XML format; the adapted skeleton is
what the reference writes for a new document.

Source: https://github.com/skerishKang/hwpx-mcp (npm: @kimdayoun/hwpx-mcp;
