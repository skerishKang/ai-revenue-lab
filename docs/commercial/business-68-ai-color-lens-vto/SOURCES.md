# B68 Sources and Evidence Register

Business: **B68 — AI Color Lens Discovery + Virtual Try-On**  
Master issue: **#3342**  
Retrieved / refreshed: **2026-10-01**

This register separates authoritative/regulatory evidence from commercial vendor claims and directional market-research estimates.

---

## Evidence policy

Use sources in this order of preference:

1. government / regulator / official statistics;
2. peer-reviewed research;
3. primary company announcements and product documentation;
4. retailer product pages;
5. commercial market research;
6. community discussions for qualitative problem discovery only.

Rules:

- market-size estimates are directional unless independently reconciled;
- vendor conversion claims are not treated as neutral evidence;
- retailer prices and assortments are time-sensitive;
- product specifications require product-level provenance;
- Reddit/social content may reveal pain points but cannot establish prevalence.

## S02 — Saudi FDA Products Classification Guidance

URL:  
https://sfda.gov.sa/sites/default/files/2024-12/classification%20guidance.pdf

Authority: Saudi Food & Drug Authority (SFDA)

Relevant classification:

- non-corrective lenses, colored or not, are considered medical devices;
- cosmetic contact lenses without medical claims must comply with Medical Device Law.

B68 use:

- regulatory boundary;
- no casual treatment of cosmetic lenses as ordinary fashion inventory;
- preference for discovery/visualization and authorized-retailer routing in V1.

Hard consequence:

B68 must not infer that a visualizer is automatically exempt from all Saudi regulatory/privacy obligations. Commercial launch requires a dedicated legal/regulatory checkpoint.

## S03 — Saudi contact-lens usage study

Title: *Use, Misuse, and Complications of Contact Lens Among the General Population of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia*

URL:  
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10825379/

Type: peer-reviewed / PMC-indexed research

Sample:
- 1,708 contact-lens users;
- 86.4% female;
- 65.7% aged 21–40.

Purpose:
- cosmetic only: 36.1%;
- medical only: 28.7%;
- medical + cosmetic: 35.1%.

Other reported facts:
- 45.8% sometimes felt discomfort;
- allergic reactions reported by 35.2%.

B68 use:

- evidence that cosmetic motivation is significant among Saudi lens users;
- safety/education should not be an afterthought.

Caveat:

This is a study sample, not a census. Do not extrapolate its exact percentages to all Saudi residents.
