# Padiem Sidecar Pricing and Commercialization

## Commercial thesis

Padiem Sidecar should be sold as an embedded AI product and integration operating layer, not as a generic FAQ chatbot.

The customer buys a supported path to place contextual Padiem AI inside an existing host product while reusing shared Engine/Core/B14 capabilities.

## Working packaging

### Sidecar Basic

Target: small site/product that needs grounded contextual assistance.

Candidate capabilities:

- branded embedded panel;
- current-page context;
- organization/site knowledge search;
- citations/evidence;
- summary/translation/rewrite;
- standard support and integration diagnostics.

### Sidecar Pro

Target: richer product workflows and private/customer context.

Candidate additions:

- file/image inputs;
- persistent continuity/memory where authorized;
- richer research;
- approved connectors/tools;
- host action proposal/confirmation;
- expanded admin/usage views.

### Sidecar Enterprise

Target: organizations requiring stronger identity/governance/integration support.

Candidate additions:

- SSO/RBAC;
- private connectors and controlled data access;
- audit/export;
- regional/private deployment options where supported;
- custom adapters;
- contractual support/SLA options;
- enterprise security/compliance review support.

These tiers are product hypotheses. Exact entitlements and names may change after customer discovery.

## Revenue structure candidates

```text
base subscription/platform fee
+ metered AI/runtime usage
+ premium connector/tool usage
+ custom adapter/integration services
+ enterprise support/private deployment
```

B53 must not invent a second authoritative usage or billing ledger. Control Plane/platform billing truth remains canonical when implemented.

## Pricing inputs required before final prices

- actual Engine/Core/B14 execution cost;
- retrieval/search/tool cost;
- storage/memory/file cost;
- support load;
- integration/adapter effort;
- customer willingness-to-pay;
- competitor/adjacent market pricing;
- expected usage distribution and abuse risk;
- gross margin target;
- private/enterprise infrastructure costs.

## Sales motions

### Self-serve

Customer creates Sidecar, registers site, selects allowed capabilities, previews and receives installation instructions.

### Assisted implementation

Padiem helps create/validate the customer adapter and context/action contract.

### Enterprise pilot

Bounded pilot with identity/private data/integration review, explicit success metrics and support plan.

## Initial value metrics

Potential customer value should be measured through actual product outcomes such as:

- reduction in navigation/search time;
- successful answer/source discovery;
- support deflection where appropriate;
- completion of target user journey;
- conversion/lead/booking assistance where applicable;
- content/document accessibility and translation value;
- operator time saved;
- user satisfaction and repeat usage.

Do not use model token volume as the primary customer value metric.

## First-party leverage

B30, B61 and LoveBud provide internal deployment evidence and reusable product learnings before external scale. Internal use must still preserve truthful cost/usage and reliability evidence.

## Competitive positioning

Position around:

```text
"AI workspace inside your existing product"
```

rather than:

```text
"another chatbot widget"
```

Differentiation should come from contextual host integration, shared research/evidence/memory/tool capabilities, action boundaries and the Padiem platform stack.
