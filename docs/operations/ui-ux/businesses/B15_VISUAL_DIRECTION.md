## Product thesis

A newsroom checks a claim against source packets and uncertainty before a human-reviewed briefing is written.

Core object: **claim dossier + source packet**, not a generic verification dashboard.

## Reserved territory — Evidence Newsroom

- editorial desk / source packet stack
- claim line at top
- source excerpts, dates, provenance and conflict marks
- uncertainty visible before briefing
- final briefing styled as newsroom copy, not engine output

Avoid giant dark poster, bias-meter dashboard, generic evidence cards and B48 engine/gate aesthetics.

## Key surfaces

- Claim intake: one claim dominates.
- Sources: source packets aligned by what they support/contradict.
- Evidence: uncertainty/conflict stays visible.
- Briefing: editorial draft derived from verified evidence.
- Review: human sign-off before release.

## Differentiation

B15 = newsroom producing a briefing. B33 = research memory/note lineage. B48 = reusable verification infrastructure/gate.

## Acceptance criteria

1. claim/source relationship is visible in first working screen;
2. source packets have real editorial/provenance hierarchy;
3. uncertainty cannot be visually hidden by the draft;
4. briefing feels like a newsroom artifact;
5. no B16/B21/B22 dark-poster collision;
6. Mobile keeps claim before sources and sources before briefing;
7. current verification contracts remain intact.
