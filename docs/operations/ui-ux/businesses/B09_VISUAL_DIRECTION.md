The current surface therefore under-delivers the promised child-facing picture-book material.

## Product thesis

A child's ordinary lived moment and caregiver intent become a continuing illustrated story in which the child's choice changes the next page.

```text
DAY → STORY MOTIF → CHOICE → NEXT PAGE → BLOOM
```

Core object: **the illustrated storybook spread**.

## Reserved visual territory

**Living Picture Book**

- warm tactile book paper, not beige SaaS canvas
- confident child-facing illustration
- paper-cut / gouache / printmaking tactility
- one recurring fictional proxy child/object motif
- asymmetric spreads and page turns
- chapter marks and folios
- one narrative accent color
- motion that extends a stable book, rather than replacing it with app animation

Avoid dark control-room/poster identity, generic e-book chrome, gamified stars/badges, worksheet UI, AI chat, and family-photo album patterns.

## Reference translation

Historical references included Wonderbly, Librio, Hooray Heroes, StoryJumper, BolognaRagazzi, Eric Carle Museum and Flying Eye Books.

Adopt:
- made-for-one-child emotional specificity
- premium cover/spread identity
- visible paper/collage tactility
- sophisticated child-facing typography
- ordinary objects transformed into narrative motifs

Reject:
- ecommerce book catalog
- avatar customizer
- classroom editor/toolbars
- direct imitation of any illustrator
- spectacle-heavy game interaction

Translate:
- the child's ordinary day visibly transforms into a fictional motif and then into a branching next page.

Verify:
- actual focal illustration dominates cover/spread screenshots
- choice is legible as a story decision, not a button grid
- Story Bloom preserves the book as the stable object
- Mobile first viewport contains title + hero art + opening story context

## Key surfaces

- **Cover:** one strong illustrated motif and personal story identity.
- **Spread:** image and Korean narrative copy share the page rhythm.
- **Lived-experience source:** shows how an ordinary object/day enters the story without exposing AI machinery.
- **Choice:** two story possibilities are visually meaningful continuations.
- **Caregiver note:** quiet marginal guidance, not a control dashboard.
- **Story Bloom:** keep the existing concept but elevate illustration quality.

## Differentiation

- B08 = family newspaper issue; B09 = child-specific illustrated narrative.
- B01 = private adult archive/publication; B09 = playful but rigorous picture-book world.
- B20 = memory-to-novel authorship; B09 = child choice alters illustrated next page.

## Acceptance criteria

A future redesign must visibly show:
1. a real picture-book spread as the core object;
2. richer illustration/tactile layers than the current sparse geometric scene;
3. clear child-choice → next-page transformation;
4. one coherent recurring motif/character system;
5. restrained caregiver marginalia;
6. Story Bloom tied to narrative meaning;
7. independently composed 390px picture-book edition;
8. no dark giant-title first-impression collision with B06/B10/B12/B18.
