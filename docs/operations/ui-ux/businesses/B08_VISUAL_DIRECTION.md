## Product thesis

A household's photos, dates, quotes and small weekly events become one finite family newspaper issue worth reading together and keeping.

```text
MOMENT → EDIT → FRONT PAGE → FAMILY SECTIONS → NEXT ISSUE
```

Core object: **the family newspaper issue**, not a photo album, chat, calendar or feed.

## Reserved visual territory

**Domestic Pressroom / Family Weekly**

- warm off-white newsprint field
- deep green-black ink
- restrained brick/coral and mustard accents
- varied story scale instead of equal cards
- masthead, issue/date/byline/caption grammar
- photo/illustration positions subordinate to edited stories
- page fold / inside-page continuity

Preserve the current light editorial field. Do not darken it to match B06 or other portfolio products.

## Reference translation

Historical B08 research used family memory products, Newspaper Club, contemporary news design and editorial storytelling.

Adopt:
- recurring finite issue identity
- strong front-page hierarchy
- lived-in but disciplined print rhythm
- family dates as an almanac, not productivity UI
- ordinary moments treated with archival weight

Reject:
- photo-upload grid
- child scrapbook/stickers
- SNS feed/reactions
- family chat
- calendar dashboard
- novelty newspaper cosplay

Verify in screenshots:
- masthead + lead story visible early
- secondary stories have deliberately different editorial roles
- mobile reads as a newspaper edition, not a stacked card feed
- page/section identity survives responsive reflow

## Key surfaces

- **Front page:** lead family story dominates; smaller signals support it.
- **Family news:** short household events use mixed editorial forms, not repeated cards.
- **Photo feature:** image sequence has captions/time/context.
- **Almanac:** dates/anniversaries read as family history and upcoming shared life.
- **Sources/provenance:** quiet and understandable.
- **Page Fold:** retain as the product-specific motion signature when final UX justifies it.
