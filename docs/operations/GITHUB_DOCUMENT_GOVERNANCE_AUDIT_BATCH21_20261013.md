A one-shot 504 or 429 supplies no model-quality judgment, and the historical AI Studio quota screenshot is neither today's free availability nor an approved PADIEM plan/price. Offline assessors do not authorize new paid provider calls.
2. **Timeouts and resource guarantees:** The old model/agent short wall-time audit was superseded by #4194. Avoid premature interruption of genuine progress while retaining independent connect/stream-idle, tool, cancellation, retry, auth and cost/rate guards.
3. **Four platform owner layers are not duplicate business units:** IP-CORE shared Tool/Evidence/Agent semantics, IP-ENGINE trusted cross-runtime service, IP-CONTROL identity/tenant/entitlement/usage and B14 Provider/model routing/credentials have distinct technical boundaries. Product-specific Chat/Claw/B66 domains stay distinct. A registered declaration is not automatically executable Production authority.
4. **Public API and open-source notices must remain public technical contracts:** Compatibility exports cannot be removed from absence of known consumers. The existing SIL OFL font license and OpenBot MIT attribution must remain where distribution requires. The pypdf optional subprocess decoder remains fail-closed without pinned binary/environment provenance; a PDF.js browser parser POC is not a legal-service launch.
5. **Customer rights and final output remain separate:** B66 synthetic fixture corpus does not store actual customer details or determine totals; extended Sol 6.1 pagination uses locked template sources but still needs owner/customer output review. B61 KRV-1961 coordinate mapping must preserve original text and check copyright, edition provenance, attribution and raw coordinate alignment before corpus release.
6. **Current availability requires fresh verification:** The in-memory Control Plane Broker M2c contract is not a public/Production PC control path, B53 Sidecar reference uses fake ports, and Engine OpenAPI source targets private Service Binding. The B54 document index includes a pricing draft pointer but does not adopt it. Historical and lifecycle navigation indexes do not supersede present-day activation or rights records.
