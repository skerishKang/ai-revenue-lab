## Cross-document governance boundaries

1. **B20 personal literary memory is distinct from B19 factual archive.** Facts, inferences, literary invention, redaction and author approval are visibly separate in a 680ms static animation. Fictional assets are original project references; neither guessed dialogue nor pseudonymized data may be represented as real third-party memories, full anonymization or approved client ingestion. Ownership, disclosure, retention and editorial consent are separate business/legal decisions.
2. **B22 personal media studio is an editorial multi-output reference, not a live publisher.** The source-to-format relay shows article, audio, video and visual card forms with human review and deliberate omission notes; the demonstrator has no real upload, generation, export, external distribution or saved media. Original SVG waveform/source claims are fabricated and must not imply documentary evidence.
3. **B33 scholarly research memory cannot invent citations.** A source fragment, equations, objections and corrections are staged as synthetic annotated scholarly memory. A final animated reviewer label is not real scholarly validation; local source-only tests were worker self-checks, not independent acceptance or a citation API.
4. **B34 dubbing and B58 writing voice need independent rights.** A synthetic voice profile and visual approval sequence do not establish the voice/speaker identity, consent, work translation rights, actual speech generation or localization quality. B58's dated copyright references correctly distinguish public-domain originals from protected modern translations, as well as one personal voice's purpose/withdrawal/deletion lifecycle. Current use of real speaker/writer assets and actual commercial licences require separately documented consent and owner/legal review.
5. **B36 safety brief and B37 route brief are NOT real emergency or geolocation systems.** They preserve uncertainty, accessibility, non-guarantee, emergency-out-of-scope and human decision labels. They neither score dangerous people/neighborhoods nor provide real 112/911 integration, crisis response, tracking, live map routing or guaranteed safe passage. Safety service copy, escalation procedures and real-user disclosures require distinct product/legal/operational review rather than treating a demo as an actual intervention.
6. **Reference-only rights are not Production rights.** All six concept sets use locally authored synthetic visual assets and derivative research notes. The cited external products and safety institutions are design/practice references only; no borrowed branded UI, official endorsement, independent audit signoff, copyright permission or general commercial licensing is established by these source artifacts.
7. **Prove the actual environment independently.** Local browser self-checks may use inline Chromium fallback when localhost navigation is blocked, and do not certify a deployed accessible site. A source-note label like HUMAN-REVIEWED is a **demo state**, not proof a person independently reviewed a real customer case.
