## Cross-product business and technical decisions

1. **Historical visual directions are not current approved production designs.** These product direction files were written against dated browser screenshots. The recurring `OWNER_UI_APPROVED=false` remains an explicit separate gate even when a source says `DIRECTION_FROZEN`, `KEEP_CORE` or `REDESIGN`. Product-specific redesign needs current source/URL evidence, an anchor/archetype screen review and Owner acceptance before a release claim.
2. **Protected review branches remain protected.** B33 Research Memory and B34 AI Dubbing Studio refer to existing review-authority work and bounded state/rights contracts. They are not authorized for automatic production merge or a global UI unification. B34 rights-first dubbing deliberately excludes real-person voice imitation or silently generated media.
3. **B36–B41 safety and emergency visuals are not operative safety services.** B36 user-owned safety plan, B37 contextual route comparison and B38 body-neutral exercise session are decision aid prototypes. B39 original/interpreted bilingual timeline is not connected to 112, B40 urgency signals never outrank human triage, and B41 confirmed emergency phrase is not a dispatch action. Actual care/route/emergency service claims would require separate qualified legal, clinical, safety and operational review.
4. **B42/B43/B45/B47/B48/B49 illustrate distinct engineering processes.** Development work queues, bounded software-work packets, content traceability, feedback aggregation, verification gates and schema mapping have different sources and human-review authority. Neither an animated seal nor demo evidence permits an automated release, opaque ranking, employee surveillance or a live public-data connector.
5. **B44 portfolio truth had an explicit browser verification blocker.** The captured Cloudflare security-check page was **not** the Portfolio Console. It cannot be counted as successful live UI conformance. Portfolio owner status, finance or strategic choices must stay separate from an automatically projected code/CI dashboard; no real business figures are reproduced here.
6. **Real customer data and commercial rights require separate decisions.** B46 personal preference profiling, B57 modern translated text/edition rights and B58 writing voice samples are demonstrable user-controlled UX concepts. This is no permission to ingest customer records, infer sensitive personal traits, publish derivative translations, imitate a real author or train a model without appropriate authorization.
7. **Mock marketplace, scheduler, SDK, fleet and archive are not active services.** B51 no one-click install/payment; B52 no running scheduled job; B53 no deployed SDK connector; B55 no live paired local devices; B59 no evidence of verified production OCR/PDF/persistent files. B59's shelf/2D reader with stable source anchor is a technically distinct product concept and visual KEEP_CORE is not current service activation.
