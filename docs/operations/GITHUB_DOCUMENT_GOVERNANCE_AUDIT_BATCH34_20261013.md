The authority/consent extent for the personal identity and current public visibility should be checked by Owner/privacy/legal review. 4. **B35 commercial service-led pilot proposal is distinct from a technical demo.** The B35 V3 product contract defines a prospective institutional buyer, diagnosis/workshop/pilot customer journey and service promise; the V3 README is simply a historical technical review index. Neither a quote, signed agreement, approved package price, live organizational deployment nor client outreach authorization is established.

Do not misclassify its title as the PADIEM Owner's actual business plan.
6. **Sensitive domains remain intentionally simulated.** Child stories, memories, family material, sports/celebrity assets, voice cloning, public-safety advice and human-review business skills are not claimed as real data or operational capabilities. Public reference descriptions must not override required future rights/identity/safety checks.
