## Consolidated governance conclusions

1. **B54 connector commercial-vs-technical boundary:** Google Drive, Gmail, Telegram, Dropbox, OneDrive, Notion, Slack and Calendar records are dated implementation/security contracts. A scope grant or an OAuth permission is not equivalent to a per-action P01 permission, a successful external send, provider receipt or Production activation. **Do not move/delete technical scope, identity, idempotency, evidence and security contracts.** SMS/Kakao include additional merchant/provider onboarding, transactional versus advertising classification, sender/recipient eligibility and commercial selection questions: those portions are mixed product policy for separate owner verification, not a license to eliminate protective controls.
2. **B60 discovery/editorial operation:** The collector requires source evidence, explicit reviewer promotion and a separate publication decision. A historic 2026-08-24 release/delegation record evidences a single dated run; it neither guarantees current model price/free-tier availability nor authorizes unrestricted automated publication. Korean copy and visual assets remain technical/editorial product artifacts. Public provenance is not evidence for a live model price.
3. **B57–B58 intellectual-property and consent:** Public-domain research, historical excerpts and demonstration rights are **not** blanket authorization to commercialize modern translations, author corpora, personal writing or voice likeness. The B58 consent terms are written for a fictional static fixture; future rights, withdrawal, training and attribution must be resolved from actual owner/customer grants before they become business commitments.
4. **B59 archival source rights:** Technical model separates immutable original sources, derived artifacts and reader position. It explicitly does not authorize external model submission without an independent user grant. Static faux-PDF is not a production renderer.
5. **B29 governance versus PADIEM company Governance:** Apartment association meeting/voting/publication UX is a **synthetic Business 29 product reference**, not actual PADIEM company corporate decisions or an electronic voting/election service. Keep its developer reference outside private business-policy decisions.
6. **B39/B40/B41 high-stakes separation:** Their emergency communications and urgency references expressly disclaim any live 112/911 connection, operator/dispatch authority, patient medical classification or emergency aid. These are safety-critical publication truths; a reference concept must not be marketed as operational emergency service.
7. **B49 public data:** DCAT/PROV/quality-provenance inspirations are visual contracts, not formal standards conformance or active authority to collect private data; private connectors belong to a different Business.
