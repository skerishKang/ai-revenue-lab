## Cross-document decisions and limits

1. **Canonical design methodology is public engineering/process evidence, not a company commercial rule.** The index, visual protocol and B01/B06 case studies explain why a good first page or a large diff is not a fully coherent site. Exact-version browser snapshots must be inspected by a person; a screenshot of a Cloudflare challenge page is not actual B44 product evidence; the owner alone approves the product's final look. B06's method may be reused, but its dark visual palette should not be cloned.
2. **Old frozen visual direction is input, not present-day automatic implementation authority.** Most B02–B32 direction files were drafted against a historical visual baseline and explicitly state `OWNER_UI_APPROVED=false`. Their `KEEP`, `FOCUSED_POLISH`, `REDESIGN` or `REDESIGN_ART_LAYER` statuses are historical proposed review verdicts, not refreshed source/runtime or owner product acceptance. The canonical current-product → visual thesis → anchor → archetype → full-surface → owner-review process controls any new implementation.
3. **Each numbered product requires distinct end-user purpose.** B02 imagery-led real destinations and adaptation differ from B04 learning feedback and B07 source-memory meaning connections; B08 family newspaper differs from B10 fan issue, B11 annotated target-language spread, B12 creator format desk, B13 private cinema ledger and B15 source-first newsroom. B16 sport timeline, B18 listening timeline, B19 memory provenance, B20 fictional draft POV, B21 strategic question memo, B22 personal multi-output story spine and B32 bounded Skill Studio cannot be collapsed into one dark poster or generic dashboard.
4. **Several product visuals touch rights and permission decisions.** B09 child/caregiver story requires real data permission and appropriate use policy before handling children. B13 video thumbnails and individual viewing records need independently established media rights/privacy. B17 actual local shop visuals, voice and publication are subject to merchant/source permissions. Other source-led concepts (B08 family photos, B10 creator likeness, B11 published passages, B19 private memory and B22 media sources) similarly gain no data or redistribution rights from being visually distinctive. No customer facts or images are copied here.
5. **Protected product review is not off-Git company Governance.** B29 *Apartment Governance* is a product-specific tutorial with its own protected Owner-approved review branch and historical `DO_NOT_MERGE` authority, not PADIEM business-policy storage. B32 Skill Studio protects a distinct unmerged product-review state machine and human approval of a proposed saved skill. This batch neither reopens nor supersedes those protected feature lines and does not enable skill installation/execution.
6. **Visual readback does not prove released services.** A source document reporting a prior web screenshot, route URI or deterministic interaction is not current live UX/consent/price/certification. Current exact-head and authenticated/unauthenticated service readback, accessibility, real feature state, product distinctions and appropriate commercial permissions remain separate owner acceptance gates.
