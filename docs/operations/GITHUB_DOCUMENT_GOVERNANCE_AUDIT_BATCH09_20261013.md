## Consolidated decisions *not* to infer from source-only evidence

1. **B14 registry and evaluation vs product entitlement.** Registration/Owner-enabled presence, a bounded synthetic provider POST, model quality test, actual first-party Engine/Claw E2E, B66 quote/PDF production and membership/Free-Plus-Pro-Max permission are separate gates. Historical 9/10/11-model reports are not current catalog. No static benchmark creates model execution grants, auto-select/default/fallback, paid provider access or confidence in current free-tier availability.
2. **Provider commercial account terms vs official models.** Kira temporary free promotion and subscription applicability, SenseNova paid call, ExLab provider capacity HOLD and Kilo temporary free routing are historical. Public promotions do not evidence actual owner billing, quota, retries, commercial/customer price or broad consent. Keep public report free of account identifiers, balances and private invoices. The exact owner ExLab manual evaluation choice and removal of an unrequested blanket data-retention refusal must not be silently undone.
3. **Historical parameter results vs latest B14 implementation.** Common `temperature=0` experiments, model-wide low output limits, older 429/504 transport failures and a superseded global parameter gate are known old evidence. Native provider options were subsequently changed: never force stale inference controls or invent server-safety restrictions from a dated test, while retaining actual auth, bounded rate/resource protection, per-action approval and exact provider-origin security.
4. **B54 executes tasks, not generic platform authentication.** Local computer trust needs Control Plane canonical pairing/identity, scoped Broker and verified device ONLINE, local physical root permission, exact material integrity, P01 consent and bounded execution. A static Connections & Devices UI or a local integration contract does not prove a deployed remote-PC action or authorize a new shared Agent/Broker framework.
5. **B54 staged source vs commercial promise.** Early product/connector documents explicitly list cloud sandbox, real connector credentials, remote device onboarding, messaging, finance read and retention storage as separate external/live gates. Draft-PR source completion is not a real paid managed hosting, legally binding retention default, published SLA or commercial plan. Planned connections center is a product UX/projection candidate, not a second authority.
6. **Stable separation remains:** B54 owns task/repository/diff/sandbox UX and domain orchestration composition. Reusable Tool/Approval/Evidence/Recovery belong to IP-CORE and Engine runtime; provider/model execution to B14; canonical identity/entitlement/credit/audit to IP-CONTROL. Keep associated public engineering contracts in GitHub, not duplicate or strip their meaning into private company Governance.
