## Cross-document boundaries and business policy candidates

1. **A work order or PR form does not grant broad implementation or company-policy authority.** The repository's CTO templates make exact source revision, role separation, feature stage, evidence class, non-goals, independent validation and release status explicit. A fast developer check is not exact-head CI green, CTO merge consent, real production readback or Owner commercial adoption.
2. **Living Travel external staging, V2 visual design and static preview are three different artifacts.** July Issue #74 source recorded a real staging exercise of Firebase/Neon/Modal/Cloudflare authentication, invitation replay, authorization and persistence; this historical proof is not today's Production, and is not the V2 owner-approved UI. Issue #457 V2 place→route→day→adapt design was held in an unmerged draft with `OWNER_UI_APPROVED=false`, pending actual Chromium/CI and renewed Owner design decision. Public travel benchmark comparisons and Pillow-gradient pictures do not establish live destinations, current opening times, reservations, or rights to unlicensed images.
3. **Personal Edition review PASS does not reverse later Owner rejection.** A July original head passed technical browser/24-screen checks; B01 V2 #443 still states DO_NOT_MERGE, and V3 #454 remains a changed art direction awaiting independent exact-head review and human Owner visual approval. Source→AI draft→editorial review→published Edition states in synthetic static preview are not evidence real participant records were processed or published.
4. **B01 raster photo rights require independent checks.** The old design-reference image plan used original geometric SVGs, while its later V5 imagery source log introduces processed photos from Pexels. The old free-use/CC0 labels and transformed WebP intent require current terms, source/photographer attribution and exclusion of readable third-party print; no blanket commercial usage or final design sign-off follows from local processing.
5. **Personal Video Archive external video metadata and private journal rights are separate.** The 11 public YouTube video source IDs/thumbnail/metadata were recorded at a specific 2026-07 date; views and availability are not live. Public linkability is not ownership of video, transcript, saved user viewing history or unrestricted thumbnail reuse. Fake LLM/discovery services and synthetic journal records in the B13 MVP do not become live YouTube ingestion, model inference or an advertising agreement.
6. **Evidence indexes are not the evidence itself.** Local before/after localhost screenshot URL manifests list intended viewport captures; they do not prove the actual image bytes were opened and inspected. A dated B13 browser verification report covers its exact local UI routes, focus and link safety only; it cannot certify current live deployment, customer opt-in or rights for later UI versions. Do not rebrand these records as independent Production E2E.
7. **Avoid product confusion and false release claims.** B02 Living Travel, B01 private literary publication and B13 private YouTube reflection archive have different user intent, customer consent and design archetypes. Historic reviewer states may inform later development but must not be converted into a shared product membership, blanket privacy consent, or revived rejected feature PR.
