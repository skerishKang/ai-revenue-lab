# Padiem Claw Business & Pricing Direction

Padiem Claw is a Korean-first execution Agent workspace: users delegate real repository/digital work and receive verified results rather than only chat responses.

## Launch model

**BYOK-first** is the recommended launch posture. Provider credentials remain in the canonical B14 credential boundary; B54 never stores provider keys in product task/run records.

Managed Padiem credits can come later through B14 + Shared Control Plane after metering, abuse controls and unit economics are proven.

## Commercial dimensions

- concurrent sandboxes
- sandbox CPU/memory/TTL tier
- background runs and scheduling
- team seats/projects
- audit/history retention
- managed model credits
- enterprise identity/network/policy controls

Potential packaging: Developer/BYOK → Pro → Team → Enterprise. Exact prices remain TBD until measured sandbox/model/support costs and competitive research are refreshed before launch.
