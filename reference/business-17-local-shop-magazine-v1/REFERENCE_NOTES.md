# Reference Notes

## Product promise

한 동네 가게의 상품, 사람, 손님 이야기, 계절과 행사를 꾸준한 발행물로 편집해 가게의 정체성과 방문 이유를 축적한다.

## Comparable products

### Popmenu

Adopted: a single venue can express dishes, events and recurring stories as one identity.
Rejected: ordering, growth metrics, automated publishing and restaurant-commerce framing.

### BentoBox

Adopted: image-led local identity and strong place-specific storytelling.
Rejected: dashboard, SEO/admin, online ordering and generic site-builder structure.

### Mailchimp content tools

Adopted: one editorial source can become email and short-form derivatives.
Rejected: scheduler, channel management, campaign metrics and publish controls.

### Square seller stories

Adopted: owner voice, craft, place and neighbourhood relationship.
Rejected: POS, loyalty, CRM and transaction-centred language.

### Independent shop journals

Adopted: tactile issue rhythm, product detail, packaging fragments and recurring folio structure.
Rejected: luxury-brand imitation, detached lifestyle styling and copied proprietary layouts.

## Visual references

- independent food and craft magazines: varied editorial rhythm, large crops, material detail;
- neighbourhood newsletters and shop-window posters: concise visit reason and local cadence;
- packaging systems: receipt, label and kraft-paper evidence as page components;
- human-scale interiors: hands, counter and repeated place details without celebrity portrait grammar.

No proprietary layout, brand mark, testimonial, copy or photography was copied.

## Direction

`Counter Journal / 카운터 저널`

Warm stock, charcoal ink, restrained shop red, receipt blue, olive and kraft. Korean-first composition with system sans and restrained system serif. Image-led but not ecommerce. Outputs look finished, but no publish or schedule controls exist.

## Boundaries

- not Neighbor Market;
- not ecommerce or POS;
- not a social scheduler;
- not CRM;
- not Creator Mini-Media with a shop logo;
- not AI chat or generic marketing SaaS.
