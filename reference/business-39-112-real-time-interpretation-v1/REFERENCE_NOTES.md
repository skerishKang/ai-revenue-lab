# Reference Notes

## Emergency communication references

1. European Commission 112 information — adopted the operator-mediated handoff and multilingual-call boundary; rejected any implication that this reference is connected to the EU 112 service.
2. Your Europe emergency-number FAQ — adopted the explicit limitation that language availability varies and that emergency numbers are not general-information services.
3. EENA public materials on emergency communication — inspected human operator, accessibility and location-uncertainty concerns; rejected proprietary operational interfaces.
4. NENA public standards summaries — inspected call-record and handoff concepts only; no dispatch workflow or standard compliance is claimed.

## Editorial and information-design references

- National Archives design system: adopted record hierarchy and visible status labels.
- Bilingual transcript and subtitle QC conventions: adopted source/draft/correction columns while rejecting media-player and caption-editor imitation.
- Readymag editorial examples: adopted asymmetric folios and restrained annotation.

## Final decisions

The visual reference is a bilingual relay ledger, not a live-call console. Machine transcript, interpretation draft, critical terms, clarification, correction and unresolved meaning remain separate. No proprietary layout, real call, agency brand, recording or operational code was copied.
