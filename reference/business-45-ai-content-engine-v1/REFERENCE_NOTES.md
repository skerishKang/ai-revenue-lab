# Reference notes

Research date: 2026-07-29

No third-party interface, brand asset, copy, illustration or document was reproduced. References were used only to identify abstract production, structure, review and accessibility patterns.

## Adjacent content-production references

### Contentful — content models and modeling patterns

- Reference: https://www.contentful.com/help/content-models/
- Reference: https://www.contentful.com/help/content-models/content-modeling-patterns/
- Adopted: content as named reusable fields and structures; reuse without coupling every output to one page layout.
- Rejected: CMS navigation, content-entry forms, publishing workflow and product branding. Phase 1 is not connected to Contentful or any CMS.

### GOV.UK — content design guidance

- Reference: https://guidance.publishing.service.gov.uk/writing-to-gov-uk-standards/plan-manage-content/understand-content-design/
- Adopted: begin with audience need, action and clear structure; make the intended user outcome visible before prose production.
- Rejected: government-service visual styling and any implication that this fictional kit is official guidance.

### Mailchimp Content Style Guide

- Reference: https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/
- Reference: https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/voice-and-tone/
- Reference: https://styleguide.mailchimp.com/writing-for-accessibility/
- Adopted: separate stable voice rules from context-sensitive tone; document terminology and accessibility constraints.
- Rejected: copying Mailchimp voice, UI, examples or brand expression.

### W3C / semantic accessibility principles

- Reference: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/reflow.html
- Reference: https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/headings-and-labels.html
- Adopted: semantic headings, labelled controls, reflow, keyboard operation and non-colour authority labels.
- Rejected: treating automated checks as complete accessibility certification.

## Editorial and production references

The composition studies the abstract visual language of print production rooms: galley proofs, crop marks, type specimens, proof sheets, editorial registers and press-check annotations. No identifiable publication layout or protected artwork was copied.

Adopted:

- one or two large editorial plates as the focal point of a state;
- visible production stages rather than a generic analytics dashboard;
- type hierarchy, proof marks and source fragments;
- draft and reviewed versions shown side by side;
- hold marks that remain visible instead of disappearing after completion.

Rejected:

- homogeneous card dashboards;
- chatbot prompts and generated-output streams;
- CMS navigation or publishing buttons;
- decorative AI gradients, model badges or provider controls;
- quantity-first “repurpose into 30 posts” language;
- visual imitation of a real magazine, newspaper or living creator.

## Product boundaries

### Structural transformation versus factual verification

Business 45 owns source-bounded structure and format rules. `FACT CHECK NOT PERFORMED` remains visible because internal consistency does not verify external facts.

### Draft versus human-reviewed variant

A generated draft can contain unsupported language. The human-reviewed variant records the correction and remains `NOT PUBLISHED`; human review does not itself grant publication authority.

### Style guidance versus living-person imitation

The kit may store broad tone, length, terminology and exclusion constraints. It does not encode an identifiable living writer’s voice or claim permission to imitate one.

### Reusable rule versus autonomous execution

The final recipe is a human-approved reusable specification. It is not a live generation instruction, provider call, scheduler, CMS automation or publication authority.

### Business 45 versus adjacent portfolio products

- Business 12 Creator Mini-Media: one creator-facing editorial package; Business 45 exposes reusable production primitives.
- Business 15 Global AI Newsroom: newsroom sourcing and verification authority; Business 45 does not perform current-event verification.
- Business 22 Personal Media Studio: personal mixed-media production environment; Business 45 is reusable content infrastructure.
- Business 46 AI Personalization Engine: user-specific adaptation; not included here.
- Business 47 Real-Time Feedback Engine: live reaction and feedback loops; not included here.
