# Reference Notes

Research date: **2026-07-27 KST**

## Product and decision-work references

### 1. Glean — enterprise search and enterprise context

- Official: https://www.glean.com/enterprise-search
- Official: https://www.glean.com/product/all-knowledge
- Pattern: internal knowledge can remain permissions-aware, current and visibly grounded in source context.
- Adopt: internal/external distinction, source attribution and freshness.
- Reject: search box, result ranking and chat as the primary product surface.

### 2. AlphaSense — market intelligence and Smart Summaries

- Official: https://www.alpha-sense.com/solutions/market-intelligence-platform/
- Official: https://www.alpha-sense.com/platform/smart-summaries/
- Pattern: every synthesis can point back to an exact source snippet, while external and proprietary internal content can coexist.
- Adopt: provenance, freshness, source-level verification and explicit limitation.
- Reject: financial-terminal density, autonomous research claims and score-like certainty.

### 3. Amazon shareholder letters — reversible and irreversible decisions

- Official: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/2016-letter-to-shareholders
- Official: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-ceo-andy-jassy-2024-letter-to-shareholders
- Pattern: a long-form executive letter can teach a repeatable decision doctrine; reversibility changes the appropriate decision speed.
- Adopt: reversibility, opportunity cost and decision posture.
- Reject: copying Amazon terminology or turning one framework into automatic authority.

### 4. Netflix Culture Memo — informed captain and farming for dissent

- Official: https://jobs.netflix.com/culture
- Pattern: one accountable decision-maker should actively seek dissent and keep disagreement visible before deciding.
- Adopt: dissent as a first-class section and explicit decision responsibility.
- Reject: using culture doctrine as a substitute for evidence.

### 5. GitLab — DRI and documented decision context

- Official: https://about.gitlab.com/blog/gitlab-inc-takes-the-devops-platform-public/
- Official: https://about.gitlab.com/blog/six-key-practices-that-improve-communication/
- Pattern: broad input can precede a clearly owned decision; the directly responsible individual remains visible.
- Adopt: named owner, review date and decision responsibility.
- Reject: task-board, status tracker and consensus workflow grammar.

## Strong editorial references

### 6. Stripe 2025 Annual Letter

- Official: https://stripe.com/annual-updates/2025
- Pattern: selective evidence, narrative pacing and a finite argument create a readable executive artifact rather than a slide deck.
- Adopt: strong title, opening synthesis, restrained section rhythm and a small number of meaningful figures.

### 7. Amazon annual-letter archive and executive correspondence

- Official: https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-ceo-andy-jassy-2024-letter-to-shareholders
- Pattern: the executive voice, operating context and strategic principle can coexist in one long-form letter.
- Adopt: correspondence identity, marginal notes and decision doctrine.

### 8. Netflix Culture Memo

- Official: https://jobs.netflix.com/culture
- Pattern: clear headings, direct prose and explicit responsibility make long-form management writing scannable without becoming a dashboard.
- Adopt: strong prose hierarchy and visible objection.

## Business 21 differentiation

Business 21 does not search the whole company, monitor the market continuously, record a past decision or generate a board presentation. It selects a deliberately small set of synthetic internal and external evidence and converges it into one finite pre-decision letter for one accountable leader. The recommendation is a temporary posture, not an autonomous decision.

## Visual decisions

- The main surface is a letter sheet with a wide annotation margin.
- Evidence appears as source slips, stamps, underlines and rules rather than uniform SaaS cards.
- Internal evidence uses textual `내부`; external evidence uses textual `외부`; colour is secondary.
- Objection red is reserved for dissent and unresolved risks.
- Decision green marks only the leader’s current posture, never objective truth.
- Serif is limited to the letter title, opening paragraph and founder quotation.
- Mobile is rewritten as one briefing page rather than a scaled-down desktop table.

## Rejected patterns

- search-result pages;
- AI chat as identity;
- KPI wall and chart dashboard;
- consulting slides and board deck;
- kanban or task management forms;
- automated optimum score;
- third-party logos, screenshots, reports or copied document layouts;
- external fonts, icon CDNs and runtime hotlinks.
