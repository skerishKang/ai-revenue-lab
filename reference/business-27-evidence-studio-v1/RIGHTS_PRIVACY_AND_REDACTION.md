# Rights, Privacy and Redaction

- Every fixture is fictional and synthetic.
- No real person's name, face, voice, address, identifier, signature, dispute, case number or private document is used.
- Redaction is represented visually; no destructive edit or export occurs.
- Party statements remain statements and are never upgraded to verified facts without source support.
- Editorial inference is labelled and may be rejected or revised by a human reviewer.
- The UI never labels a person guilty, fraudulent, violent, dishonest, a perpetrator or a victim.
- The folio is a preview only: `NO OFFICIAL FILING OR SUBMISSION`.
- AI-assisted text remains `AI-ASSISTED DRAFT · HUMAN REVIEW REQUIRED`.
