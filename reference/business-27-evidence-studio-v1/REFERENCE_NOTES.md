# Reference Notes

Research date: 2026-07-28. References are used only for pattern analysis; no screenshots, logos, protected copy or layouts are reproduced.

## Adjacent products and workflows

### CaseFleet
- Official references: https://www.casefleet.com/features/timelines and https://support.casefleet.com/en/articles/9595939-facts-and-citations-explained
- Adopt: facts linked directly to citations, chronology as a primary review surface, visible distinction between a fact record and its supporting source.
- Reject: litigation-preparation positioning, AI assistant, trial-readiness claims and full case-management workflow.

### LexisNexis CaseMap Cloud
- Official references: https://law.lexisnexis.com/casemap-cloud and https://www.lexisnexis.com/Casemapsuitesupport/cm/cm14/about_facts.htm
- Adopt: known, potential and undated facts can coexist; facts connect to issues, people and documents; uncertain dates remain explicitly unresolved.
- Reject: legal evaluation scores, automated case strategy, cloud dashboard and attorney-centric product chrome.

### Relativity aiR for Case Strategy
- Official reference: https://www.relativity.com/data-solutions/air/case-strategy/
- Adopt: structured, reviewable chronologies and narrative links back to material.
- Reject: automated legal narrative generation, e-discovery scale, persuasive case-story framing and AI-first interface.

### U.S. National Archives archival description
- Official references: https://www.archives.gov/research/catalog/lcdrg/archival-materials and https://www.archives.gov/research/catalog/lcdrg/elements/object-identifier
- Adopt: provenance, access restrictions, date ranges, record types and meaningful unique object identifiers.
- Reject: public catalogue navigation and collection-management software chrome.

## Editorial references

### Harry Ransom Center identity by Pentagram
- Reference: https://www.pentagram.com/work/harry-ransom-center
- Adopt: contemporary institutional authority, strong type, archive material as active evidence.
- Reject: copying marks, fonts, palette or exhibition layouts.

### Readymag Design Stories
- Reference: https://readymag.com/designstories
- Adopt: timelines, captions, folio rhythm, large editorial pauses and asymmetric archive composition.
- Reject: decorative novelty, draggable interaction and motion without evidentiary value.

## Final visual metaphor

`Evidence Reconstruction Room / 증거 복원실`: a quiet case editor's table combining source sleeves, timestamp strips, claim ledgers, redaction overlays and a restrained submission folio.

## Product separation

This is not legal advice, a police database, a surveillance product, Apartment Governance, Company Memory or Decision Archive. It covers one bounded disputed incident and preserves disagreement rather than determining liability.
