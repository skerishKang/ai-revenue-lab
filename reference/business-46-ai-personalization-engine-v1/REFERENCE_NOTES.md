# Reference Notes

Adopted: explicit consent, editable preferences, temporary-context separation, candidate comparison, explanations, fallback, override/reset. Rejected: hidden tracking, ad/political targeting, protected inference, psychographic scoring, automatic winner, addictive feed. Product boundary follows Issues #287 and #292.

Primary references: NIST AI RMF (transparency/accountability), OECD AI Principles (human-centered values), EU GDPR Art. 5 and Art. 22 concepts (purpose limitation and automated-decision safeguards), UK ICO guidance on AI and data protection, W3C privacy principles. Used only for hierarchy and boundary language, not legal advice.
