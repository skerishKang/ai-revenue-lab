# Reference decisions

## Product promise

흩어진 회사 기록을 증거가 연결된 조직 기억으로 바꾸어, 무슨 일이 있었고 왜 그랬으며 누가 무엇을 알고 있었고 이후 무엇이 달라졌는지를 보여준다.

## Comparable products inspected

### Glean

Adopted: cross-source context, explicit provenance, and the principle that evidence must remain connected to claims.

Rejected: search-bar-first framing, connector marketplace imagery, answer-chat dominance, and productivity dashboard composition.

### Guru

Adopted: visible verification, ownership, freshness, and governed citation status.

Rejected: uniform knowledge-card grids, current-policy-only framing, and answer-bot chrome.

### Atlassian Confluence

Adopted: version history as context and structured hierarchy as a reference model.

Rejected: page trees, editor chrome, whiteboards, template libraries, and generic wiki navigation.

### Notion Enterprise

Adopted: connected company knowledge and project context.

Rejected: all-in-one workspace framing, database-table aesthetics, productivity monochrome, and AI chat as the main surface.

### Corporate heritage and archive practice

Adopted: archival description, corporate history as a strategic resource, collection metadata, oral history, and narrative routes through records.

Rejected: anniversary nostalgia, public museum exhibition as the whole product, and decorative history without operational consequence.

## Editorial references inspected

### Readymag Design Stories

Adopted: magazine-scale typography, timeline rhythm, image-caption systems, and editorial pacing across multiple media types.

Rejected: novelty interaction that weakens source readability.

### Harry Ransom Center identity by Pentagram

Adopted: contemporary institutional authority, typographic contrast, and an archive identity that is serious without feeling dusty.

Rejected: direct copying of marks, typefaces, or layouts.

### Keepthinking narrative archives

Adopted: layered collection structure, narrative routes, and the combination of imagery, metadata, and chronology.

Rejected: collection-management software chrome and public-catalogue neutrality.

## Final visual metaphor

`Institutional Memory Atlas / 회사 기억 아틀라스`

The composition combines a private archive, engineering folio, evidence ledger, corporate chronicle, and oral-history record. The screen language uses ledger rules, folio numbers, document edges, blueprint overlays, registry marks, marginal notes, and large editorial pauses.

## Adopted visual rules

- archive ivory background with carbon navy ink;
- oxidized copper for age and continuity;
- blueprint cyan for engineering lineage;
- registry red for contradiction, restriction, and final human review;
- muted grey-green for verified continuity;
- large Korean serif titles, restrained sans reading text, monospace evidence metadata;
- asymmetrical spreads rather than repeated cards;
- visible labels for contemporary records, later recollections, verified facts, editorial inference, contradiction, missing evidence, and later consequence.

## Explicitly rejected patterns

- purple AI gradients;
- neon glow and glassmorphism;
- AI mascotography;
- generic node graphs;
- fake KPI panels;
- file-manager layouts;
- search-first dashboard composition;
- identical rounded cards;
- seamless AI-authored historical narrative.
