Quorum correction

The synthetic flow requires this path:

quorum-incomplete → attendance-open → attendance supplement → manual recheck → quorum-recorded or quorum-incomplete

Direct quorum-incomplete → quorum-recorded is forbidden. The result is a manual fixture record, not a legal judgement.
