# Reference Notes

## Product references

1. **HOAmeeting** — adopted the visible progression from agenda to motion, vote and approved minutes. Rejected drag-and-drop workflow, exports and operational meeting management.
2. **Condo Control** — adopted the idea that meeting packets connect agenda, current financials and open items. Rejected dues, reconciliation and property-management ERP surfaces.
3. **CondoVoter** — studied notice, meeting and election sequencing. Rejected real ballots, identity verification, live tallying and the election-platform identity.
4. **HOA Flow** — adopted dated resident notices linked to meetings, deadlines and prior communication. Rejected automated delivery and communication operations.
5. **Public HOA document libraries** — adopted chronological document naming and approved-versus-current distinctions. Rejected the plain download-directory presentation.

## Editorial and archival references

1. **The National Archives Design System** — adopted restrained institutional hierarchy, readable tables, clear statuses and accessible content structure; rejected a government portal clone.
2. **Readymag Design Stories** — adopted editorial pacing, archive-like folios and varied compositions under one system; rejected decorative interaction that obscures records.
3. **Cooper Hewitt identity system** — adopted a precise but flexible grid that can handle labels, folders, tickets and long-form editorial material; no identity assets were copied.
4. **Sotheby's editorial archive approach** — adopted contextual object/document storytelling and catalogue-like historical continuity; rejected luxury branding.

## Visual decision

`Resident Assembly Ledger / 주민총회 원장`: notice paper, meeting ledger, rulebook tabs, stamped resolution, expenditure sheets, contract sleeve, election envelope, complaint-response correspondence and audit annotations.

Palette: warm notice paper, graphite ink, forest green, brick red and muted brass. System serif for document titles and long records; system sans for controls and metadata; monospace for record IDs and dates.

## Adopt / reject summary

Adopt: agenda-to-resolution continuity, resident-readable public notices, approved-versus-proposed status, rule citations, dissent retention, public/private ribbons, record IDs and audit annotations.

Reject: generic dashboard tiles, rounded-card wall, accounting graphs, payment controls, facility monitoring, legal case language, community feed, AI chat and real election mechanics.

## Product boundaries

Business 29 is recurring apartment governance. It is not Business 27 disputed-evidence reconstruction, Business 28 general decision preservation, Business 5 resident commerce, a property ERP, an accounting product or an electronic voting system.
