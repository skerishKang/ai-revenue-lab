## Adopted visual grammar

- A fictional atlas occupies the first viewport and remains spatially stable.
- Evidence enters as rails, ledger rows, and annotated fragments rather than equal dashboard cards.
- The lead story dossier is always identifiable by headline, verification state, last review, uncertainty, and responsible editor.
- Mineral paper and evidence sheets interrupt the dark operations table, producing an editorial-room rather than SaaS-console identity.
- Colour is restricted to source links and four evidence states: unresolved, contradiction, corroborated, and human approved.
- Korean is primary. English is used as secondary operational notation.
- Mobile abandons the full control-room layout and focuses on one dossier, one map excerpt, verified facts, uncertainty, latest change, and next check.

## Rejected visual grammar

- Generic dark SaaS cards and KPI tiles.
- Cybersecurity SOC severity walls.
- Stock-market tickers and trading-terminal density.
- AI agent node graphs, provider selectors, API keys, or chat chrome.
- Purple gradients, neon glow, glassmorphism, animated globes, particles, and route-flight spectacle.
- Rich-text editor, publish button, content calendar, kanban board, analytics dashboard, or administration settings.

## Why the final direction is distinct

The interface is neither a consumer feed nor a publishing CMS. It treats a story as a reviewable evidence dossier. Geography establishes where signals originate; evidence rails show what agrees or conflicts; edition and handoff sheets preserve uncertainty and editorial responsibility. The synthetic fixture prevents a polished static reference from being mistaken for a live newsroom system.
