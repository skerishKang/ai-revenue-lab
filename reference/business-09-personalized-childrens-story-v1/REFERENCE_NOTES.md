# Reference Notes

Research date: 2026-07-26  
Scope: rapid visual-pattern research for a Phase 1 UI reference only.

## Adjacent products

### Wonderbly — Personalized Books

- URL: https://www.wonderbly.com/
- Observed pattern: a child becomes the narrative center through a premium, made-for-one-book proposition.
- Adopted: clear single-story identity, prominent cover art, emotionally specific title treatment.
- Rejected: commerce-first catalog framing, product grids, purchase funnel, and template-personalizer controls.

### Librio — Personalized Children’s Books

- URL: https://librio.com/en/
- Observed pattern: gentle family tone, restrained illustration, gift-quality physical-book positioning.
- Adopted: quiet warmth, tactile paper cues, caregiver relevance without control-panel aesthetics.
- Rejected: shopping taxonomy, gift merchandising, and product-detail repetition.

### Hooray Heroes — Personalized Books

- URL: https://hoorayheroes.com/
- Observed pattern: protagonist personalization and family-oriented keepsake language.
- Adopted: recurring proxy character and a strong “this story belongs to one child” signal.
- Rejected: avatar-customizer emphasis, sales urgency, and broad novelty-product styling.

### StoryJumper — Children’s Storybook Creation

- URL: https://www.storyjumper.com/parents
- Observed pattern: children and caregivers can create or co-create books.
- Adopted: the idea that an everyday fragment can become a story artifact.
- Rejected: editor canvas, creation toolbar, social publishing, classroom framing, and authoring workflow.

### Remento — Family Story Book

- URL: https://www.remento.co/
- Observed pattern: lived memories are transformed into a durable narrative object.
- Adopted: quiet provenance marks and the dignity of ordinary memories.
- Rejected: interview workflow, adult memoir orientation, recording prompts, and family archive dashboard.

### Flying Eye Books

- URL: https://flyingeyebooks.com/about-flying-eye/
- Adopted: contemporary editorial illustration, strong color discipline, generous spreads, and sophisticated child-facing typography.
- Rejected: reproducing any publisher title, cover, spread, or proprietary illustration.

## Adopted visual system

- A wide, book-like stage rather than an application dashboard.
- Deliberate image/body asymmetry instead of repeated equal cards.
- One recurring fictional child proxy: a small figure in a rust-red raincoat.
- Everyday objects become fictional motifs: yellow rain boots become cloud-crossing equipment; a library receipt becomes a paper forest trail; a seed becomes a night lantern.
- Korean-first narrative copy with chapter marks, folios, and quiet inspiration dates.
- Warm off-white paper, ink navy, moss green, rain blue, ochre, and one narrative accent: vermilion.
- Imperfect SVG edges, grain masks, pencil paths, and layered shapes to suggest gouache, printmaking, and collage without reproducing a commercial book.

## Rejected visual system

- Generic e-book reader chrome, chapter lists, font controls, and progress bars.
- Children’s game UI: quests, rewards, stars, badges, levels, streaks, scores, or oversized choice buttons.
- Learning worksheet patterns: rubrics, checkboxes, assessment charts, or classroom dashboards.
- Family album patterns: photo grids, dates as metadata cards, or real-child identity cues.
- AI chat patterns: bubbles, prompt fields, assistant avatars, sparkle icons, purple gradients, or model-status labels.
- SaaS patterns: KPI cards, sidebar navigation, analytics, account menus, or uniform rounded containers.

## Product boundary versus Business 3 · Living Fiction

Business 3 is a general-reader platform for shared and personal branching fiction. Its center of gravity is narrative participation and broader reader-created worlds. Business 9 is a caregiver-and-child picture-book result whose visual world quietly transforms one fictional proxy child’s ordinary day into a continuing illustrated story. This Phase 1 reference does not expose branching logic, prompts, public sharing, authoring, or generation internals.

## Mobile adaptation

The mobile state is not a scaled desktop spread. It becomes a vertical picture-book edition with the title, hero art, and opening paragraph visible in the first 390×844 viewport. Review controls collapse to previous/next so the book remains visually dominant and no horizontal navigation strip is required.
