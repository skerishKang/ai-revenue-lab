# Reference notes

Research date: 2026-07-29

This file records principles only. No third-party interface, branding, map, message, document or illustration was copied.

## 1. UN Women — Safe Cities and Safe Public Spaces

Source: https://knowledge.unwomen.org/en/digital-library/publications/2025/12/safe-cities-and-safe-public-spaces-for-women-and-girls-global-initiative-third-international-compendium-of-practices

Adopted:
- locally relevant, user-centred and partnership-oriented response framing;
- autonomous mobility and women-led decision-making;
- visible distinction between evidence, local context and intervention choice.

Rejected:
- city-scale programme metrics as an individual danger score;
- any inference that a location or person is dangerous.

## 2. SAMHSA — Trauma-informed approaches

Source: https://www.samhsa.gov/mental-health/trauma-violence/trauma-informed-approaches-programs

Adopted:
- safety, trust, collaboration and empowerment as service qualities;
- resist re-traumatisation by avoiding accusatory or victim-blaming copy;
- user control over what is recorded and shared.

Rejected:
- clinical diagnosis, treatment framing or medical authority;
- forcing disclosure to advance the interface.

## 3. GOV.UK Design System — Accessibility

Source: https://design-system.service.gov.uk/accessibility/

Adopted:
- keyboard-operable review controls and visible focus;
- explicit text labels rather than colour-only authority;
- responsive reflow and 390px containment;
- reduced-motion information equivalence.

Rejected:
- assuming a component library alone proves accessibility;
- inaccessible dense dashboard compression.

## 4. National Domestic Violence Hotline — Safety planning and trusted support

Sources:
- https://www.thehotline.org/what-is-a-safety-plan/
- https://www.thehotline.org/resources/tips-for-safely-reaching-out-for-support/

Adopted:
- personalised, practical planning;
- trusted-person check-in with an agreed response to a missed check-in;
- minimal, user-controlled sharing and clear communication boundaries.

Rejected:
- copying hotline advice into an automated recommendation engine;
- covert tracking, broad data collection or automatic law-enforcement contact.

## 5. National 911 Program — emergency-service boundary

Source: https://www.911.gov/calling-911/

Adopted:
- explicit distinction between planning software and an emergency channel;
- persistent `EMERGENCY RESPONSE OUT OF SCOPE` label;
- no simulation of dispatch, call-taking or responder instructions.

Rejected:
- panic countdowns, emergency-call UI or dispatch-console aesthetics;
- any claim that this reference can provide emergency assistance.

## Adopted visual patterns

- advocacy-desk folio rather than incident dashboard;
- user-account and observable-context sheets;
- interpretation and missing-evidence separation;
- equal-authority bounded option cards;
- trusted-contact strip and accessibility note;
- evidence-preservation boundary card;
- human-reviewed handoff memo and response-brief seal.

## Rejected patterns

- red alert spectacle, siren iconography and countdown pressure;
- crime map, threat score, offender profile or dangerous-area ranking;
- CCTV, facial-recognition, tracking or police-dispatch aesthetics;
- chatbot-centred composition;
- actual messages, case records, addresses, people or victim imagery.

## Product relationship boundaries

### Difference from AI Safe Route

AI Safe Route compares bounded route alternatives with visible environmental uncertainty. Business 36 coordinates a broader personal-safety response brief, trusted-contact plan, accessibility constraints and escalation handoff. It does not generate or rank routes.

### Difference from Evidence Studio

Evidence Studio structures existing material into a reviewable evidence record. Business 36 begins with user-led safety planning and only marks lawful evidence-preservation boundaries. It does not make forensic or legal findings.

### Difference from emergency dispatch

Emergency dispatch receives an active emergency request and coordinates responders. This static reference has no call, alert, location, responder, medical or police execution. `EMERGENCY RESPONSE OUT OF SCOPE` remains visible in the final brief.

### Difference from surveillance products

Surveillance products may collect live location, identity, camera, sensor or communication data. This reference collects none of those. Trusted-contact coordination is a bounded human agreement, not monitoring.
