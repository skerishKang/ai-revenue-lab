# Reference Notes

Research date: 2026-07-27 KST

## Product references

1. **Remento — How it works / Inside our books**
   - Official: https://www.remento.co/how-it-works
   - Official: https://www.remento.co/our-books
   - Adopt: voice and photograph paired with a book chapter; original recording remains reachable; chapter-scale continuity.
   - Reject: automated polished prose as the default truth layer. Business 19 shows source boundaries, uncertainty and competing recollections directly.

2. **Storyworth — What is Storyworth**
   - Official: https://welcome.storyworth.com/what-is-storyworth
   - Adopt: human-scale recurring prompts, photograph/story pairing, durable memoir rhythm.
   - Reject: year-long prompt workflow and printing commerce because Phase 1 is UI-only and Business 19 is an evidence-aware book artifact.

3. **Meminto — Biography / Life Book**
   - Official: https://meminto.com/product/write-a-biography/
   - Official: https://meminto.com/topics/life-book/
   - Adopt: category-led memory continuity and multimedia references.
   - Reject: broad template catalogue, gift language and generic scrapbook treatment.

4. **Library of Congress — Photographic Evidence**
   - Official: https://guides.loc.gov/local-history-and-family-photos/photographic-evidence
   - Adopt: evaluate photographs like textual evidence, seek original records, weigh context, and distinguish conclusion from source.

5. **Library of Congress — FSA/OWI cataloging and uncertain dates**
   - Official: https://www.loc.gov/pictures/static/data/fsa/resources/cataloging.html
   - Adopt: visible approximate-date notation, date ranges and uncertainty marks rather than silent precision.

## Editorial and archival references

- Smithsonian Institution Archives digitization metadata: source, creator, title and rights fields stay close to the image object.
- Smithsonian photographic log books: negative number, subject, people, photographer and dates function as a compact provenance ledger.
- Documentary contact sheets: use sequencing and frame identifiers to show that a selected photograph belongs to a larger evidence set.
- Archival review sheets and finding aids: present unresolved provenance and undated items without inventing continuity.

## Visual decisions

- Page stock, ink, annotation red and date blue replace generic AI gradients.
- Serif is restricted to chapter openings and quotations; system sans handles evidence labels.
- Photographs remain fixed while provenance appears in the `Provenance Reveal` motion.
- Exact, estimated, conflicting and unresolved statuses use distinct labels and never rely on colour alone.
- The mobile state reduces the page to one photo, one date confidence, one quotation and one source note.

## Rejections

- social feed grids;
- floating scrapbook cards;
- parallax, dust, glow and page-flip spectacle;
- invented dialogue or reconciled memories;
- external fonts, stock photography and runtime CDNs;
- form workflows that would imply accepted UX.
