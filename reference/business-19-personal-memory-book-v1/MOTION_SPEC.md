# Signature Motion — Provenance Reveal / 출처 드러남

Target duration: **680ms** (within the required 640–720ms range).

## Sequence

1. Chapter photograph stays fixed.
2. Date annotation appears at 90ms.
3. Place annotation appears at 170ms.
4. Source lines extend toward the page margin at 250–310ms.
5. Uncertainty note appears at 390ms.
6. Family-review mark appears last at 540ms.

## Constraints

- no page flip;
- no floating photographs;
- no dust particles;
- no parallax scrapbook;
- no glow;
- motion is CSS-only and deterministic;
- replay control is keyboard accessible.

## Reduced motion

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` makes all annotations, source lines, uncertainty and review marks immediately complete with effectively zero duration.
