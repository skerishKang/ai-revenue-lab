# Reference notes

Research date: 2026-07-27

Scope: current product and visual-reference research for a static Phase 1 UI. No proprietary screen, logo, article copy, uniform, player likeness or league mark is reproduced.

## Comparable products

### Apple Sports / My Sports
- Official sources: https://support.apple.com/guide/apple-sports-app/apple-sports-app-at-a-glance-apdec6a74b4c/web and https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/05/apple-sports-expands-to-more-than-90-new-countries-and-regions/
- Adopted: followed teams placed first, fast fixture hierarchy, compact lineup/formation cues and direct viewing context.
- Rejected: ecosystem dependence, real-time score identity, Live Activities, streaming hand-off and betting odds.

### OneFootball
- Official sources: https://company.onefootball.com/ and https://company.onefootball.com/contact/
- Adopted: personalised club focus and a clear match/news hierarchy.
- Rejected: broad football portal density, advertising grammar, transfer-rumour feed, prediction markets, commerce and social mechanics.

### FotMob
- Official source: https://www.fotmob.com/en/download
- Adopted: detailed match context, television schedule awareness and concise player/match evidence.
- Rejected: live-score utility dominance, hundreds-of-leagues breadth, rankings, predictor and dense data-table grammar.

### The Athletic
- Reference basis: followed-team editorial depth, match analysis and season-long narrative continuity.
- Adopted: an authored reason to watch, uncertainty language, reflective recap and season arc.
- Rejected: subscription-news homepage, copied article layout, publication navigation and paywall conventions.

### Official match-centre patterns
- Adopted: fixture priority, verified-result distinction, lineup/tactical hierarchy and key-moment sequence.
- Rejected: rights-controlled broadcast imagery, official league branding, sponsor inventory and generic scoreboard identity.

## Strong visual references

### Society for News Design
- Official sources: https://snd.org/about-us/ and https://snd.org/best-of-design-competitions/
- Adopted: visual journalism that combines editorial hierarchy, graphics, photography and restrained annotation.
- Rejected: recreating any awarded entry or newsroom identity.

### ESPN visual storytelling and Cover Story
- Official sources: https://espnpressroom.com/press-release/espn-cover-story-franchise-launches-today-with-features-across-digital-linear-social-platforms/ and https://www.espnfrontrow.com/2023/05/revolutionizing-basketball-storytelling-disneys-real-time-tech-crystallizes-espns-shot-chart-visualizations/
- Adopted: magazine-cover impact, spatial diagrams and motion used to explain a decisive sequence.
- Rejected: broadcast chrome, network branding, live automation and spectacle-first animation.

### Olympic design and data storytelling
- Sources: https://www.theolympicdesign.com/ and https://metalab.harvard.edu/p/records/
- Adopted: disciplined pictorial systems, long-range season/history continuity and micro-to-macro storytelling.
- Rejected: Olympic marks, historical identities, medal-table dashboard grammar and immersive installation effects.

### Matchday programmes and tactical notebooks
- Adopted: ticket fragments, issue numbers, narrow rules, annotated pitch marks, kickoff stamps and tactile editorial layering.
- Rejected: club marketing copy, sponsor blocks, merchandise promotion and nostalgic imitation.

## Adopted visual grammar

- Night-pitch ink and chalk-paper contrast.
- Image-led editorial spreads rather than uniform dashboard cards.
- Stadium atmosphere, tactical field, player silhouette, ticket fragment and season strip as repository-local originals.
- Korean-first copy with secondary English fixture labels.
- Condensed system display treatment for time, score and fixture labels; system sans for body copy.
- Fine rules, crop marks, handwritten-style notes and restrained coral turning-point emphasis.
- One 680ms Turning Point Sweep that preserves layout and scroll position.

## Rejected product grammars

- Generic live-score table, standings database or notification utility.
- Betting odds, wagers, fantasy roster optimization or prediction market framing.
- Club-owned app, sponsor wall, real crest, uniform or player likeness.
- News portal, transfer-rumour feed or Fan Magazine-style celebrity profile.
- Generic AI gradient, glass-card grid, chat box or generated-content controls.

## Business boundary

- **Business 16 · Personal Sports:** match-cycle and season-cycle notebook for a small set of followed teams and players.
- **Business 10 · Fan Magazine:** culture/editorial publication around a favourite creator, performer or athlete; not fixture-led.
- **Business 6 · World Feed:** broad global/local story discovery; not a durable fan relationship.

## Resulting direction

The reference reads like a completed personal matchday edition laid out after editorial preparation. It explains why this fixture matters, what to watch, what changed the match and what question carries into the next viewing date—without becoming a live service or official club product.
