# Reference notes

Research date: 2026-07-26  
Time box: rapid pre-implementation review, below 20 minutes  
Purpose: visual and product-boundary research only; no third-party layout, copy, branding, or asset was copied.

## Adjacent products

### 1. FamilyAlbum

- URL: https://family-album.com/en
- Adjacent category: private family photo and video sharing
- Adopted pattern: family-only emotional tone and chronological memory value
- Rejected pattern: upload-first album navigation, feed-like image stream, baby-centric visual identity
- Boundary decision: this reference begins with an edited issue and article hierarchy, not a gallery or upload surface.

### 2. Tinybeans

- URL: https://tinybeans.com/
- Adjacent category: private family journal, photo sharing, and printed keepsakes
- Adopted pattern: small ordinary moments deserve durable preservation
- Rejected pattern: social reactions, comment loops, uniform journal entries, child scrapbook styling
- Boundary decision: short family events use different editorial treatments instead of repeated post cards.

### 3. Remento

- URL: https://www.remento.co/
- Adjacent category: family story preservation and keepsake books
- Adopted pattern: source material is transformed into a finished artifact with archival weight
- Rejected pattern: single-person life-story focus, prompt-driven interview flow, book-order journey
- Boundary decision: the output is a recurring household newspaper issue assembled from mixed weekly records.

### 4. FamilyWall

- URL: https://www.familywall.com/
- Adjacent category: shared family organizer and calendar
- Adopted pattern: household dates can become a shared family signal
- Rejected pattern: productivity dashboard, task management, dense utility calendar, location and messaging features
- Boundary decision: dates are presented as a living almanac with anniversaries and shared plans, not as scheduling software.

## Chosen visual grammar

- Direction: `Domestic Pressroom / 가족 편집국`
- Core artifact: a premium but lived-in weekly family newspaper
- Typography: system serif display face for headlines, system sans for factual details and captions
- Color: warm off-white paper, deep green-black ink, coral red, and mustard yellow
- Density: editorial rather than app-like; dominant lead story followed by intentionally different secondary forms
- Image treatment: repository-local synthetic editorial illustrations in photo positions, each with a caption and timestamp
- Signature motion: 680ms Page Fold that retains a masthead/location rail

## Explicit product distinction

### Not a photo album

The image is subordinate to an edited story, caption, sequence, issue number, byline, and publication date. There is no upload grid or album browser.

### Not a family chat

Short quotations appear as edited pull quotes. There is no composer, message thread, reaction, typing status, or reply behavior.

### Not an SNS feed

No repeated post card, avatar, like count, algorithmic stream, or infinite scroll is present. Every item has a page-level editorial role.

### Not a calendar app

The monthly view emphasizes anniversaries and shared plans as a household almanac. There is no event creation, drag-and-drop, reminders, or productivity chrome.

### Not a children's scrapbook

The palette is restrained, decorative edges are limited, and sticker motifs are absent. The visual tone addresses the whole family and treats ordinary life as an archival publication.
