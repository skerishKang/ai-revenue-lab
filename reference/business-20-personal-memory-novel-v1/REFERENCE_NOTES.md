# Reference Notes

Research date: 2026-07-27

## Product boundary

The product transforms selected memories into a private literary scene while preserving explicit distinctions between confirmed fact, inference, fictionalisation, redaction and author-approved text. It is not a factual memory book, Living Fiction, prompt-to-novel generator, writing chatbot, style-imitation product, screenplay editor or publishing marketplace.

## Comparable product and research references

### StoryTerrace

Official product and process pages describe a professional memoir workflow built around memory prompts/questionnaires, interviews, outline review, first-chapter calibration, manuscript review, design and delivery. Adopted: source gathering before prose, staged manuscript review, authorial feedback and book-proof seriousness. Rejected: ghostwriting-service positioning, commercial packages, printing and publication workflow.

- https://www.storyterrace.com/how-it-works
- https://www.storyterrace.com/blog/writing-a-memoir

### StorySage

The 2025 research system separates interviewing, session notes, planning and section writing across a multi-agent framework. Adopted: source collection and narrative construction are distinct stages. Rejected: conversational autobiography workflow, persistent multi-session ingestion and autonomous generation, all outside Phase 1.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14159

### Scrivener

Official materials present editor, corkboard, outliner and manuscript views as different lenses on the same writing project. Adopted: scene-level structure and bird's-eye transformation map. Rejected: copying its application chrome, project tree or index-card UI.

- https://www.literatureandlatte.com/scrivener/overview
- https://www.literatureandlatte.com/learn-and-support/video-tutorials/mastering-scriveners-view-modes

### Ulysses

Official materials emphasize a restrained writing environment, modular sheets, outline/navigation and revision-focused metadata. Adopted: quiet manuscript surface, modular scene treatment and secondary metadata. Rejected: library/sidebar replication, goals, statistics and publishing integrations.

- https://ulysses.app/en/
- https://help.ulysses.app/en_US/getting-started/details-and-tips

### Privacy and redaction

ICO guidance distinguishes pseudonymisation from anonymisation and stresses that transformed identifiers can remain personal data. Privacy International also recommends redacting unnecessary personal data and cleaning document metadata. Adopted: visible `REDACTED` states, no claim that redacted material is anonymous, and permanent separation of private details from the literary passage.

- https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/data-sharing/anonymisation/pseudonymisation/
- https://privacyinternational.org/key-resources/4887/1-risk-assessment

## Editorial and visual references

- Annotated manuscript proofs: restrained correction marks, marginal notes, folios and visible revision layers.
- Critical editions: primary text and apparatus occupy distinct typographic zones.
- Contact sheets and archival finding aids: source images are grouped with provenance instead of used as decoration.
- Literary magazines: serif reading text, controlled measure and asymmetrical image-led spreads.

No existing book cover, living author's visual identity, product screenshot, typeface binary or third-party illustration is copied.

## Adopted visual system

- Warm proof paper and manuscript ink rather than generic white SaaS cards.
- Source blue, inference amber, fiction violet-brown, redaction red and approval green as semantic margin rules.
- Korean-first interface copy with restrained English folios.
- One atmospheric original winter-station image, then document and diagram assets.
- State review tabs remain visually explicit as review controls, not product navigation.

## Rejected patterns

- AI chat composer and prompt box
- typewriter effect or page flip
- floating paper, ink particles and AI glow
- writing streaks, word counts, scores or gamification
- publication marketplace, printing controls and collaboration
- false certainty about remembered dialogue or third-party details
