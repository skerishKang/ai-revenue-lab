# Signature Motion — Fact-to-Fiction Fold

## Purpose

Show that a literary scene is assembled in ethically distinct layers rather than generated as one undifferentiated block.

## Duration

Nominal sequence: **680ms** (within the required 640–720ms range).

## Sequence

1. `0ms` — factual anchor chips and factual paragraph remain fixed.
2. `120ms` — inference paragraph enters as a separate amber-labelled layer.
3. `240ms` — fictional passage reveals horizontally with a violet-brown margin rule.
4. `380ms` — redacted paragraph appears, but the redacted fragment remains fully obscured.
5. `480ms` — closing literary sentence appears.
6. `600ms` — author approval note appears last.
7. `680ms` — the stage resolves to a stable complete state.

## Interaction boundary

The replay button exists only to inspect the signature motion. It does not edit text, generate prose, save state or establish an approved UX flow.

## Reduced motion

`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables the sequence and renders the complete layered state immediately. The redaction remains visually obscured in both modes.

## Prohibited effects

No typewriter input, page flip, floating paper, ink particle, parallax spectacle or AI glow.
