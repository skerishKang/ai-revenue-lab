# Reference notes

Research date: 2026-07-27

## Product references

### Google NotebookLM — Audio Overviews

Official sources reviewed:

- https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16212820
- https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16179559
- https://support.google.com/notebooklm/answer/16215270

Adopted:

- source-grounded listening artifact;
- explicit source selection and citation visibility;
- background-listening context and chapter-like orientation;
- synthetic-output disclosure.

Rejected:

- notebook/chat as the primary visual object;
- broad research workspace framing;
- interactive AI-host conversation.

### Wondercraft — podcast and audio editor

Official sources reviewed:

- https://www.wondercraft.ai/podcast
- https://www.wondercraft.ai/feature/audio-editor
- https://www.wondercraft.ai/feature/podcast-editor

Adopted:

- clear segment composition;
- readable sequence and duration structure;
- simple editorial hierarchy around audio.

Rejected:

- multitrack production timeline;
- drag-and-drop editing controls;
- voice cloning, effects, publishing and collaboration workflows.

### ElevenLabs Reader and Studio

Official sources reviewed:

- https://elevenlabs.io/docs/help-center/product/distribution-publishing/eleven-reader/what-is-eleven-reader
- https://elevenlabs.io/docs/projects/audio-native
- https://elevenlabs.io/studio

Adopted:

- conversion of long-form reading material into a listenable experience;
- chapter-aware long-form structure;
- compact mobile reading/listening hierarchy.

Rejected:

- voice-library spectacle;
- production editor, timeline and export controls;
- real-person voice likeness, cloning and generated sound effects.

## Accessibility reference

W3C WAI transcript guidance:

- https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/transcripts/

Adopted:

- persistent transcript excerpt;
- meaningful source label adjacent to spoken text;
- keyboard-visible review controls;
- `prefers-reduced-motion` completion state.

## Editorial and visual references

The composition also studies general public-radio programme guides, literary magazine issue covers, printed travel journals and postal ephemera. No third-party screens, artwork, logos or copy are included in repository assets.

Patterns adopted:

- one dominant edition cover rather than a dashboard;
- paper/ink contrast, programme-guide rules and serif editorial hierarchy;
- time and chapter sequence as quiet marginalia;
- archive organized by listening moment rather than performance metrics.

Patterns rejected:

- streaming-platform carousel cloning;
- card-heavy productivity dashboards;
- fake analytics, creator-growth metrics and podcast distribution controls;
- neon waveform spectacle, equalizers, spinning records, avatars, particles or 3D audio objects.

## Product boundary

The artifact is a recurring personal audio edition. It is not NotebookLM, a DAW, a voice studio, a podcast host, a generic AI chat or a reduced Personal Media Studio.
