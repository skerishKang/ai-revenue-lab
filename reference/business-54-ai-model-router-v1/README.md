## Product result

The wholly fictional Miso Research Office fixture classifies one Korean supplier-risk briefing task, applies hard privacy and quality constraints, reviews three fictional domestic/external/local candidates, records exclusions and availability, and produces a human-approved routing policy without making any model call.
