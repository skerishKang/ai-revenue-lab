# Reference Notes

## Adopted
- W3C PROV: explicit provenance and derivation boundaries.
- NIST AI RMF: visible uncertainty, documented limits and human oversight.
- GOV.UK service guidance: plain-language status, authority and accessible focus.
- FDA human-factors principles: conspicuous execution boundaries and use-error prevention.
- Qualitative research synthesis practice: preserve disagreement rather than manufacturing consensus.

## Rejected
Social-feed replication, sentiment heatmaps, employee dashboards, automatic winner badges, engagement optimization, moderation queues, removal controls, live counters and opaque scores.

## Distinction
Business 45 owns content production and Business 46 owns durable personalization. Business 47 only organizes authorized feedback into a reviewable, execution-withheld action record. It is not analytics, moderation, monitoring or autonomous production control.
