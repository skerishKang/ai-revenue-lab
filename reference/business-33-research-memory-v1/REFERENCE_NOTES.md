# Reference Notes

## Visual proposition
**Research Constellation Desk / 연구 기억 별자리**: source fragments, annotations, equation context, claim/evidence threads, objections, revisions and unresolved questions remain connected over time.

## Comparable products
1. **Zotero PDF Reader and Note Editor** — adopted source-location anchoring and source context; rejected library/file-manager layout, ingestion and citation production.
2. **Hypothesis** — adopted passage-anchored commentary and explicit metadata; rejected social discussion and live annotation behavior.
3. **ResearchRabbit** — adopted visible source relationships; rejected search, recommendation and neon citation graphs.
4. **Connected Papers** — adopted a bounded constellation around an origin question; rejected algorithmic discovery and bibliographic completeness claims.
5. **OpenAlex** — adopted separation of work/source authority; rejected live API and metadata authority.

## Editorial and archival references
1. **The National Archives Design System** — restrained typography, line-length discipline and explicit record labels; no institutional branding.
2. **Wellcome Collection archives** — drafts, notebooks and reference material as durable research records; no archive search UI or real imagery.
3. **Europeana** — provenance and rights awareness; no search-result grid or cultural-heritage branding.
4. **Cooper Hewitt digital collection practice** — documentation of born-digital change history; no museum identity or object grid.

## Adopted
Korean-first scholarly typography, question folio, source/interpretation separation, readable variables, retained objections, dedicated mobile brief.

## Rejected
Zotero clone, generic note app, card grid, chatbot, fake citation authority, neon network, real paper screenshots or journal-page imitation.

No proprietary visual copying.
