# Reference Notes

Research date: 2026-07-29

Phase 1 uses public-health and accessibility references only for visual hierarchy, plain-language intensity, user choice and boundary language. No guideline amount is applied to Jiyu as individualized medical advice or prescription.

## Adopted patterns

- “Some activity is better than none” becomes a non-punitive progression model.
- Activity may be broken into smaller units; the session strip therefore uses visible time blocks and checkpoints rather than a single performance score.
- The CDC talk test informs plain-language exertion wording (“short sentences possible”) without heart-rate, calorie or biometric claims.
- Accessibility guidance informs preference-first options, quiet/home context, equipment alternatives, gradual starting points, and respectful body-neutral language.
- Human review is shown as a correction slip and next-review timestamp, not automated certainty.

## Rejected patterns

- Weekly public-health targets as an individualized prescription.
- Disease prevention claims as a session outcome.
- Calorie-burn rankings, body-fat scoring, appearance comparison and punitive streaks.
- Automated form scores, camera skeletons, static-image certification and biometric authority.
- Mandatory progression or regression framed as failure.
- Instructions to continue through pain, dizziness, sudden breathlessness or uncertainty.

## Product boundaries

### Difference from Personal Sports

Personal Sports is sports-media and fan-channel work. Business 38 is a bounded personal movement-session planning and human-review reference. It does not produce sports news, fandom content or performance media.

### Difference from medical rehabilitation

This reference does not diagnose, treat, rehabilitate, prescribe clinical exercise, screen medical suitability or determine the cause of discomfort. Unknowns remain visible and health concerns are redirected to qualified professionals.

### Difference from camera pose products

There is no camera, pose estimation, skeleton overlay, joint-angle calculation, repetition tracking or form certification. Static SVGs are explicitly marked as visual references only.

### Difference from weight-loss applications

The primary result is a reviewable adaptive movement plan, not weight change. There is no calorie target, scale, body transformation image, appearance score, body-shaming copy or outcome guarantee.
