# Reference notes

Research date: 2026-07-26

Scope: rapid reference research for a static Phase 1 visual direction. No third-party screen, logo, copy, or asset is reproduced.

## Adjacent products

### Ghost for Publishers
- URL: https://ghost.org/publishers/
- Adjacent value: independent publishing, membership, web publication, and newsletters in one publishing product.
- Adopted: strong publication identity, issue hierarchy, and a creator-owned editorial tone.
- Rejected: CMS administration, member management, analytics, publishing controls, and revenue surfaces.

### Substack
- URL: https://substack.com/
- Adjacent value: creator publication and newsletter distribution centered on an individual voice.
- Adopted: author voice as the organizing identity and a readable issue-like presentation.
- Rejected: social network feed, subscriber mechanics, compose workflow, and distribution controls.

### beehiiv
- URL: https://www.beehiiv.com/
- Adjacent value: newsletter creation, web publishing, growth, and monetization.
- Adopted: clear newsletter edition structure and subject-line emphasis.
- Rejected: growth dashboard, subscriber counts, analytics, ad network, automation, and monetization UI.

### Typefully
- URL: https://typefully.com/
- Adjacent value: focused short-form writing and cross-platform adaptation.
- Adopted: concise format-specific copy and visible differences in length and rhythm.
- Rejected: scheduling calendar, platform logos, collaboration, publishing actions, analytics, and AI-chat affordances.

### Descript
- URL: https://www.descript.com/
- Adjacent value: script-led video and audio creation.
- Adopted: alignment between spoken narration and visual beats.
- Rejected: timeline editing, playback chrome, upload/render controls, waveform UI, and production tooling.

## Editorial and design references

### The Pudding
- URL: https://pudding.cool/
- Adopted: a strong issue index, story-specific visual systems, and editorial material that does not look like generic SaaS.
- Rejected: data-story interaction patterns that would imply a finished interactive product.

### The Creative Independent
- URL: https://thecreativeindependent.com/
- Adopted: independent-publication voice, restrained typography, generous reading rhythm, and creator-centered authorship.
- Rejected: interview/archive information architecture and publication-wide browsing.

### Newspaper Club
- URL: https://www.newspaperclub.com/
- Adopted: tactile paper proportions, crop marks, page numbering, print-production cues, and visible differences among formats.
- Rejected: ecommerce, print ordering, templates, pricing, and production fulfillment.

### It's Nice That
- URL: https://www.itsnicethat.com/
- Adopted: editorial density, bold type, image-led composition, and varied article proportions.
- Rejected: category-heavy publication navigation, advertising, and feed replication.

## Adopted visual grammar

- Warm paper background, black ink, signal red, and proofing blue.
- One source idea distributed into four materially different editorial forms.
- Page numbers, crop marks, source slips, timestamps, underlines, and selective proof marks.
- Overlapping physical-paper geometry instead of uniform cards.
- Korean-first copy with a quiet English secondary label system.
- A single 660ms Format Relay that changes headline, crop, and production marks while preserving desk orientation.

## Rejected product grammars

- **CMS:** no content tree, editor toolbar, save/publish state, media library, or admin navigation.
- **Scheduler:** no calendar, queue, time slots, channel connections, or publish buttons.
- **Kanban/project management:** no task columns, progress states, assignees, checklists, or workflow stages.
- **Marketing dashboard:** no followers, impressions, open rate, clicks, conversion, funnel, or growth claims.
- **AI chat:** no prompt field, chat bubbles, model selector, assistant persona, or generated-response loop.

## Boundary from adjacent Businesses

- **Business 12 · Creator Mini-Media:** one creator, one source idea, four focused editorial forms, and a compact desk metaphor.
- **Business 22 · Personal Media Studio:** a broader personal production environment spanning text, audio, video, books, and a wider creation system. Business 12 deliberately avoids that studio breadth.
- **Business 45 · AI Content Engine:** an internal reusable production capability for multiple products. Business 12 exposes no engine, nodes, pipelines, provider routing, or generation controls.

## Resulting direction

The reference should read as a completed independent editorial desk being reviewed after production—not as software used to manage production. The creator's voice and the physical evidence of editing are the primary interface.
