# Reference Notes

Research date: 2026-07-28

## Comparable products and records

### Atlassian Confluence decision / DACI templates

- Official: https://www.atlassian.com/software/confluence/templates/decision
- Adopt: a decision has status, impact, involved people, due date and outcome.
- Reject: generic workspace chrome, editable form grammar, dashboards and collaboration controls.

### Loomio proposals and outcomes

- Official: https://help.loomio.com/en/user_manual/polls/proposals/index.html
- Official outcome guidance: https://help.loomio.com/en/user_manual/polls/outcomes/index.html
- Adopt: proposals surface responses and new information; outcomes explicitly state the decision made.
- Reject: voting UI, threaded discussion, consensus scoring and live group workflow.

### GitLab architecture decision records

- Official: https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/engineering/architecture/workflow/
- Adopt: lightweight, durable decision records preserve rationale beside long-lived documentation.
- Reject: engineering-only terminology, repository navigation and implementation workflow.

### Notion decision-log templates

- Official marketplace collection: https://www.notion.com/templates/collections/top-9-free-decision-making-tool-templates-in-notion
- Adopt: clear objective and structured alternatives can reduce ambiguity.
- Reject: database table, kanban, template-marketplace and productivity-dashboard grammar.

## Editorial and visual references

### MACK

- Official: https://mackbooks.co.uk/pages/about-us
- Adopt: materiality, care in edition-making, restrained book-object presentation.
- Reject: art-book commerce, product catalogue and direct layout imitation.

### The Gentlewoman

- Official: https://thegentlewoman.co.uk/
- Adopt: strong issue identity, disciplined type hierarchy, image-and-text rhythm.
- Reject: fashion imagery, personality-profile treatment and brand imitation.

### Actual Source

- Official: https://actualsource.work/categories?cat=actual-source
- Adopt: archival category marks, compact captions, edition-like numbering.
- Reject: client-brand assets, proprietary marks and retail design language.

### The New York Review of Books

- Official: https://www.nybooks.com/
- Adopt: dense but readable long-form hierarchy, archive continuity, visible editorial seriousness.
- Reject: newspaper front page, publication branding and article-copy imitation.

## Resulting direction

**Decision Ledger Room / 결정 원장실** uses paper folios, hard vertical rules, reason brackets, dissent margins, evidence receipts and stamps. It deliberately avoids rounded-card SaaS, transcript/chat, kanban, CRM, legal case-file and analytics patterns.
