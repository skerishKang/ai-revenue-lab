# Reference research and visual decisions

## Research date

```text
2026-07-27 (Asia/Seoul)
```

Only current official product or publisher sources were used for product-surface observations. No screenshots, logos, editorial pages, protected copy or proprietary layouts were copied into the implementation.

## Comparable products

### Descript

- Official sources:
  - https://www.descript.com/tools/video-to-text
  - https://www.descript.com/tools/video-clip-maker
  - https://www.descript.com/video-editing
- Observed pattern: one audio/video source becomes an editable transcript and multiple derivative outputs; text editing maintains a visible relationship to underlying media.
- Adopted: clear source continuity and the idea that derivatives remain connected to a selected source moment.
- Rejected: transcript-first application chrome, podcast identity, dark timeline imitation, export/publishing workflow and AI co-editor framing.
- Business 22 application: the adaptation state fixes one source note in place while article, audio, video and card treatments appear around a master-story annotation.

### Canva Magic Resize / Magic Switch

- Official sources:
  - https://www.canva.com/pro/magic-resize/
  - https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/magic-studio/
- Observed pattern: one design can be transformed into visibly different formats, with a clear taxonomy of destination media.
- Adopted: immediate recognition that each destination has a different format and proportion.
- Rejected: blank-canvas start, social-size grid, template marketplace, generic brand-kit UI and one-click magic language.
- Business 22 application: the suite uses distinct editorial geometry—long galley, cue sheet, storyboard, contact sheet, folded newsletter and book signature—instead of equal cards.

### Adobe Express for Business

- Official sources:
  - https://business.adobe.com/products/express-business/create-standout-content.html
  - https://business.adobe.com/products/express-business.html
- Observed pattern: consistent identity can be carried across document, presentation, social and video outputs.
- Adopted: shared identity across materially different media classes.
- Rejected: template picker, universal editor, scheduler, channel-resize utility, stock-asset catalog and enterprise dashboard language.
- Business 22 application: proof paper, graphite field, process blue, vermilion annotation, source labels and folios create a single editorial family without forcing identical containers.

### Castmagic

- Official source:
  - https://www.castmagic.io/
- Observed pattern: several content derivatives are visible in parallel from one audio/video source.
- Adopted: parallel visibility and explicit derivative naming.
- Rejected: quantity theatre, “hundreds of assets” claims, upload funnel as product identity, marketing automation and ready-to-post language.
- Business 22 application: seven finite proofs are shown as an editorial set; no output counts, automation metrics or publishing claims appear.

### Riverside

- Official sources:
  - https://riverside.fm/solutions/podcasters
  - https://riverside.fm/video-editor
  - https://riverside.fm/product
- Observed pattern: transcript, show notes, clips, audio and video are medium-specific parts of one production context.
- Adopted: timecodes, cue structures and storyboard treatment that make audio/video feel native to their media.
- Rejected: recording studio, waveform editor, podcast publishing workflow, remote guest identity and playback controls.
- Business 22 application: audio is a visual cue sheet and video is a four-scene proof; neither is playable or editable.

## Editorial references

### Readymag editorial examples

- Official source: https://readymag.com/examples/editorial
- Observed pattern: asymmetrical composition, typography-led hierarchy and visual anchors guide reading through large editorial surfaces.
- Adopted: deliberate asymmetry, scale contrast and substantial negative space.
- Rejected: interaction spectacle, portfolio-showcase chrome and effects that obscure the product object.
- Business 22 application: the cover and suite read as editorial proof walls rather than an app dashboard.

### Readymag Design Stories

- Official source: https://readymag.com/designstories/
- Observed pattern: distinct editorial chapters share one strong identity while each chapter uses its own composition.
- Adopted: a common editorial system with different page geometries.
- Rejected: copying any designer story, biography, image, type treatment or specific layout.
- Business 22 application: each state is a chapter in the same studio, but sources, spine, suite, adaptation and trace have separate spatial logic.

### SOFA Magazine digital/print case study

- Official source: https://readymag.com/customers/sofamagazine
- Observed pattern: print and digital can reinforce one editorial identity while using medium-specific expression; the medium should serve what is being narrated.
- Adopted: proof-paper surfaces inside a digital field and differentiated print-like/digital forms.
- Rejected: magazine-brand imitation, decorative editorial styling without provenance, interaction as spectacle and direct reuse of SOFA’s visual language.
- Business 22 application: article/book/newsletter surfaces use reading-oriented paper geometry, while audio/video use time and scene structures.

## Final visual decisions

### Visual metaphor

```text
Master Edition Room / 마스터 에디션 룸
```

A private cross-media editing room built around one bounded source corpus. The central product object is the **Media Edition Set**, not a content dashboard, file uploader or template catalog.

### Typography

- Korean-first system sans for controls and annotations;
- system serif stack for article, book and editorial statements;
- limited monospace for source IDs, timecodes, folios and provenance.

No external font or icon CDN is used.

### Color

- graphite/studio black for the room;
- warm proof paper for reading surfaces;
- restrained process blue for source continuity;
- vermilion for editorial intervention and exclusions;
- muted signal yellow only for unresolved decisions and timing.

### Density and hierarchy

- desktop: a wide editorial room with large proof surfaces and asymmetrical clusters;
- tablet: proofs reorganize into two- and one-column reading groups;
- mobile: a separate “today’s edition set” sequence rather than a scaled proof wall.

### Image plan

All visual assets are repository-local original SVGs. They are synthetic, non-identifying and documented in `IMAGE_SOURCES.md`.

### Product boundaries preserved

- not Business 12: no compact creator posting desk or channel cadence;
- not Business 18: audio is one proof among seven;
- not Businesses 19/20: no memory-preservation or literary-transformation framing;
- not Business 21: no strategy advice or executive decision letter;
- not Business 45: this is a user-facing product identity, not a reusable production engine;
- not Business 58: no voice model, corpus, consent or licensing layer.
