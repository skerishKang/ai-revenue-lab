# Reference notes

## Comparable operating systems

1. **Slurm job scheduling documentation** — adopted the separation between job intent, resource eligibility and execution state; rejected terminal and cluster-console imitation.
2. **Kubernetes scheduling and taint concepts** — adopted explicit eligibility/ineligibility and quarantine semantics; rejected Kubernetes visual language, live cluster topology and control-plane affordances.
3. **NVIDIA DCGM health concepts** — adopted evidence-oriented health labels; rejected live telemetry, vendor branding and benchmark claims.
4. **SLSA provenance vocabulary** — adopted checksum/provenance evidence as a release precondition; rejected supply-chain certification claims.
5. **Incident command ledgers** — adopted bounded retry, named authority and persistent unresolved state; rejected emergency-service branding.

## Editorial and visual references

1. **NASA Graphics Standards Manual** — adopted disciplined labels, plates and technical-document hierarchy; rejected logo and identity replication.
2. **Massimo Vignelli transit diagrams** — adopted controlled line weight and systems legibility; rejected route-map/switchyard treatment reserved for Business 54.
3. **The Pudding explanatory graphics** — adopted annotated evidence-first storytelling; rejected scrolling narrative complexity.
4. **Information is Beautiful Awards archives** — adopted concise visual comparison and visible provenance; rejected decorative data spectacle.

## Product distinction

Business 55 is not Business 54. Business 54 decides which candidate route policy fits one task. Business 55 defines a human-approved local fleet operating plan for devices, local models, planned jobs, capacity estimates, incidents, quarantine and release. It does not invoke or route a model.
