# Reference Notes

Research was time-boxed to a focused direction pass before implementation. No third-party screen, asset, copy, or layout was copied.

## Adjacent products reviewed

### TheBrain
- URL: https://www.thebrain.com/
- Relevant pattern: a selected item keeps neighboring context visible; scale and adjacency communicate importance.
- Rejected: luminous node-web aesthetics, technical knowledge-network language, dense radial graph behavior.

### Kumu
- URL: https://www.kumu.io/
- Relevant pattern: relationships can be read through spatial grouping, hierarchy, and guided presentation rather than a flat list.
- Rejected: social-network analysis framing, graph metrics, generic node-link diagrams.

### Heptabase
- URL: https://heptabase.com/
- Relevant pattern: spatial placement of mixed notes and visual fragments can support non-linear understanding.
- Rejected: infinite whiteboard productivity shell, editable card canvas, note-authoring controls.

### Milanote
- URL: https://milanote.com/
- Relevant pattern: image, note, and material fragments can coexist in an irregular but legible editorial field.
- Rejected: moodboard/work-management chrome, draggable board metaphor, repeated utility cards.

### The Memory App
- URL: https://thememoryapp.com/
- Relevant pattern: memory can be understood as a collection with spatial and temporal context.
- Rejected: journal capture workflow, literal geographic memory map, AI recall/search positioning.

## Editorial, cartographic, museum, and archive references

### David Rumsey Map Collection
- URL: https://www.davidrumsey.com/
- Adopted: warm archival ground, index marks, provenance, image fragments, map-sheet hierarchy.
- Rejected: catalogue-first search density and historical-map reproduction as the product itself.

### Cooper Hewitt · Watercolor Maptiles by Stamen Design
- URL: https://watercolormaps.collection.cooperhewitt.org/
- Adopted: cartography can privilege atmosphere and material texture without losing orientation.
- Rejected: live geographic navigation and map-tile interaction.

### Atlas of Design
- URL: https://atlasofdesign.org/
- Adopted: maps as authored cultural pages, restrained palettes, meaningful variation in scale and annotation.
- Rejected: literal one-map-per-page replication.

### Subjective Editions / Subjective Atlas approach
- URL: https://www.bureaudevet.be/subjective-editions
- Adopted: personal experience may be mapped through selective labels, drawings, fragments, and editorial sequencing.
- Rejected: illustration-only storytelling without readable temporal or relational structure.

## Adopted visual grammar

- Warm paper ground with archival ink and muted mineral/botanical accents.
- Cartographic rules, contour lines, coordinate strings, index tabs, date stamps, and marginalia.
- Unequal scale: current meaning dominates without a numeric score.
- Mixed forms: image slips, typed records, map labels, object silhouettes, and handwritten-style notes.
- Context-preserving selection: related pieces surface while the surrounding atlas remains visible.
- Korean-first copy with ordinary explanations instead of graph terminology.

## Rejected patterns

- Dark canvases with glowing points and filaments.
- Galaxy, constellation, neural-network, hologram, or AI-brain metaphors.
- Uniform rounded cards and generic dashboard grids.
- Ranking weights, centrality, confidence scores, or fake analytics.
- Full-screen geographic maps, route controls, pins, and turn-by-turn semantics.
- Contact profiles, social feeds, CRM fields, family-tree generations, and editable note canvases.

## Product distinction

### Not a family tree
People are only one material type. A place, event, or object may be larger than a person, and there is no lineage or generation hierarchy.

### Not a map app
Coordinates and contours provide orientation, but no route, live location, pan/zoom, or geographic accuracy is promised. Place is treated as remembered context.

### Not a mind map
There is no central topic with uniform branches. Relationships are editorially composed from lived significance, dates, and evidence fragments.

### Not a note graph
The screen does not expose backlinks, nodes, graph controls, technical labels, or an authoring canvas. It presents a private atlas page, not a database visualization.
