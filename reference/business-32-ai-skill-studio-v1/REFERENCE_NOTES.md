# Reference Notes

## Visual proposition

`Operational Training Bench / 업무 실습대` combines task briefs, industrial work instructions, evidence clips, reviewer markup and a controlled version stamp. It deliberately avoids generic SaaS dashboards, LMS course grids, chatbot heroes, kanban boards, workflow marketplaces and agent-builder diagrams.

## Comparable public products

### Scribe

Official source: https://support.scribehow.com/hc/en-us/articles/8951146003741-New-User-Guide

Adopt: concise numbered process steps, visible capture-to-document transformation, editable alerts and tips.

Reject: automatic screen capture, live browser extension, sharing and analytics. This Phase 1 reference is static and repository-local.

### Tango

Official source: https://crm.tango.us/process-documentation

Adopt: assistance close to the work, compact annotations and clear how-to sequencing.

Reject: overlay execution, browser automation and in-product assistance.

### Process Street

Official sources:
- https://www.process.st/help/docs/document-approvals/
- https://www.process.st/help/approval-history/

Adopt: evidence stays with the work; approval, rejection, comments and version history remain visible; exceptions are preserved.

Reject: automation, integrations, permissions, notifications and production workflow routing.

### Dozuki

Official source: https://www.dozuki.com/solutions/need/work-instructions

Adopt: standardized work instructions, visual task breakdown, version-controlled procedure and explicit deviations.

Reject: manufacturing analytics, qualification tracking and live operational data.

### Trainual

Official source: https://trainual.com/documentation

Adopt: ownership, process documentation and reusable organizational knowledge.

Reject: LMS, course completion, certificates, employee tracking and knowledge-search UI.

## Strong information-design references

### GOV.UK task-list and step-by-step patterns

Official source: https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/task-list-pages/

Adopt: bounded tasks, explicit completion state, plain-language labels.

Reject: government visual identity and portal navigation.

### U.S. Web Design System process list

Official source: https://designsystem.digital.gov/components/process-list/

Adopt: strong ordered sequence and accessible text-first hierarchy.

Reject: direct component styling or brand replication.

### IBM Carbon structured content

Official source: https://carbondesignsystem.com/components/data-table/usage/

Adopt: dense evidence comparison with strong column alignment and explicit status labels.

Reject: enterprise dashboard chrome and product-specific component styling.

## Originality boundary

No third-party logo, screenshot, image, copy block or proprietary layout is included. References informed hierarchy, evidence adjacency, review marks and procedural density only.
