# Reference notes

## Comparable operating systems

1. **Slurm job scheduling documentation** — adopted the separation between schedule definition, run state and evidence retention; rejected terminal and cluster-console imitation.
2. **Kubernetes CronJob semantics** — adopted explicit schedule/cadence/expiry concepts and condition-watch pattern; rejected live cluster topology, controller loops and monitoring dashboards.
3. **Transactional idempotency patterns** — adopted idempotency key and duplicate-run prohibition; rejected payment or order-processing visual language.
4. **ITSM change management ledgers** — adopted bounded retry, exception ledger, pause authority and human approval gates; rejected service-desk or ticketing branding.
5. **Observability runbooks** — adopted evidence-strip and suppressed-notification patterns; rejected live telemetry, metric dashboards and alert routing.

## Editorial and visual references

1. **Swiss railway timetable design** — adopted disciplined cadence bands, exact/flexible time windows and legible schedule plates; rejected railroad-brand identity.
2. **NASA mission operations documentation** — adopted run-state trace, exception ledger and human-sign-off visual language; rejected agency replication.
3. **Edward Tufte data-ink principles** — adopted evidence-strip density and annotation-first legend; rejected decorative chart junk.
4. **Information is Beautiful comparison graphics** — adopted concise retry/notification boundary communication; rejected animated infographic complexity.

## Product distinction

Business 52 is not Business 51. Business 51 distributes workflow marketplace listings. Business 52 governs one scheduled operation from definition through human-approved runbook without executing, scheduling or delivering anything. It does not distribute, market or publish workflows.
