# Web CTO Vision Review

A vision-capable reviewer opened the desktop, tablet and mobile seven-state montages and the dedicated 390px listing captures directly.

## Verdict criteria

- `Curated Workflow Market Hall` identity is immediate and coherent.
- The composition is not a generic app-store grid, prompt-card wall, plugin directory or SaaS dashboard.
- Package, workflow, compatibility, evidence and listing states read as different editorial objects within one system.
- `PERMISSION REQUIRED — NOT GRANTED` is conspicuous and never appears granted.
- `DEPRECATED VERSION — DO NOT INSTALL` is visually separated from `CURRENT VERSION 1.4.0`.
- `LISTED PRICE — SYNTHETIC` reads as a static placard, not a purchase or checkout action.
- Publisher claims remain visually separated from independent validation.
- Human review is the central authority; marketplace approval is explicitly not legal or security certification.
- No screen implies live execution, installation, account connection, permission grant, payment or completed transaction.
- The 390px listing brief remains readable without horizontal overflow.

The exact reviewed Git SHA and final deployment-readiness verdict are recorded in the Draft PR conversation after independent exact-head validation. This review does not authorize Ready, merge, Issue closure, UX or backend work.
