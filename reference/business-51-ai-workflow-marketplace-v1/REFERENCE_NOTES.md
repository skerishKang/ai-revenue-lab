# Reference Notes

Research date: 2026-07-29. References were used for structural and editorial analysis only. No runtime assets, copied interface text, brand marks or third-party screenshots are included.

## Comparable workflow products

1. **n8n Workflow Templates** — workflow discovery through task naming, node structure and template detail. Adopted: making the workflow object legible before use. Rejected: import-first framing and community-volume emphasis.
   - https://n8n.io/workflows/
2. **Zapier Workflow Gallery** — outcome-led workflow descriptions and creator attribution. Adopted: clear use-case naming and publisher visibility. Rejected: live automation launch cues and broad gallery density.
   - https://zapier.com/workflow-gallery
3. **Make Templates** — scenario templates with connected service context. Adopted: input/output relationship visibility. Rejected: immediate “Try it” execution framing.
   - https://www.make.com/en/templates
4. **Bardeen Playbooks** — reusable playbook concept and task-oriented naming. Adopted: workflow as a bounded reusable object. Rejected: create/run affordances and prompt-led automation creation.
   - https://support.bardeen.ai/hc/en-us/articles/23925126983053-Creating-Playbooks

## Editorial and catalog references

1. **Are.na Editorial / Information Systems** — blocks and channels as explicit information units. Adopted: evidence inserts and modular provenance objects.
   - https://www.are.na/editorial/information-systems
2. **Are.na Editorial essays** — editorial rhythm built from collected source blocks rather than a dashboard grid. Adopted: generous reading areas and object-led sequencing.
   - https://www.are.na/editorial/category/essay
3. **A24 Shop books and zines** — object-specific cover presentation and strong editorial merchandise identity. Adopted: package cover as a focal artifact. Rejected: purchase CTA and commerce signaling.
   - https://shop.a24films.com/collections/books/
4. **Monocle Shop** — curated market-hall hierarchy across print objects and travel publications. Adopted: shelf/placard vocabulary, compact object metadata and restrained catalog density.
   - https://monocle.com/shop/

## Adopted visual grammar

- warm paper hall rather than a dark SaaS dashboard;
- one large package cover instead of a generic app-store grid;
- task-route poster for ordered workflow structure;
- perforated compatibility and permission tickets;
- split evidence insert to prevent publisher claims from reading as verified facts;
- version bands that make deprecated status visually unmistakable;
- review seal whose text explicitly denies legal/security certification;
- synthetic price placard without a transaction button.

## Rejected visual grammar

- uniform plugin cards;
- prompt-card walls;
- star ratings, downloads, popularity ranks or “best seller” claims;
- crypto tokens, neon marketplace aesthetics or speculative pricing;
- install, connect, buy, checkout, run, publish or grant-permission controls;
- universal compatibility badges;
- green “verified” treatment applied to publisher claims;
- SaaS navigation, KPI tiles or analytics charts.

## Product distinction

Business 51 is a distribution and review layer for bounded workflow packages. Business 48 owns verification components, Business 50 owns private-data connections, Business 32 owns skill creation, and Business 52 owns scheduled operation. This Phase 1 reference does not implement any of those runtime capabilities.
