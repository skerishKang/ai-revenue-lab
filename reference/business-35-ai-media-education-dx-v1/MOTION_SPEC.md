# Baseline-to-Operating-Playbook Motion

## Contract

`idle|complete → running → complete`

Replay removes previous running/complete classes, enters idle, forces style recalculation, then restarts. Normal completion authority is the actual `animationend` event from `.playbook-seal` where `animationName === "playbookComplete"`. No fixed timeout is used.

## Sequence

1. current-state evidence
2. skill-gap diagnosis
3. policy constraint
4. training module
5. facilitator correction
6. workflow revision
7. pilot evidence
8. unresolved risk retention
9. `HUMAN-APPROVED MEDIA DX PLAYBOOK`

Nominal final end: 780ms (`640ms` delay + `140ms` duration). Replay 1/2 must resolve to equivalent computed styles and geometry. Focus and scroll remain stable. Reduced motion immediately enters the same information-complete state. `POLICY CONSTRAINT`, `PROHIBITED USE`, and `UNRESOLVED ADOPTION RISK` remain visible after completion.
