# Reference Notes

## Comparable transformation and learning references

1. **Design Council — Framework for Innovation** — adopted the evidence-first, define, develop and bounded-test cadence; rejected direct reproduction of the Double Diamond graphic. Source: https://www.designcouncil.org.uk/resources/framework-for-innovation/
2. **OECD — Developing skills for digital government** — adopted competence frameworks, role-specific learning and evaluation boundaries; rejected public-sector branding and performance claims. Source: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/developing-skills-for-digital-government_f4dab2e9-en.html
3. **OECD Framework for digital talent and skills** — adopted the distinction between environment, skills and workforce change; rejected generic maturity dashboards. Source: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/the-oecd-framework-for-digital-talent-and-skills-in-the-public-sector_4e7c3f58-en.html
4. **OECD Digital Government Outlook 2026 — AI adoption and governance** — adopted broad literacy plus role-specific depth and practical/ethical constraints; rejected country statistics as product fixture. Source: https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/2026/06/digital-government-outlook_4585678e/full-report/adopting-and-governing-ai-in-government_7ef312a9.html
5. **Microsoft Learn change-management strategy** — adopted early engagement, coaching and workflow-support distinction; rejected healthcare-specific product and clinical copy. Source: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/industry/healthcare/dragon-copilot/solution-planning/change-management-strategy

## Editorial and information-design references

1. **Design Council Double Diamond** — adopted visible progression from evidence to bounded delivery; rejected proprietary visual composition.
2. **National Archives Design System** — adopted document hierarchy, restrained institutional typography and explicit labels; rejected government-portal replication. Source: https://design-system.nationalarchives.gov.uk/
3. **Cooper Hewitt digital collection** — adopted dense collection-wall rhythm and provenance-like annotations; rejected museum branding and object cards. Source: https://collection.cooperhewitt.org/
4. **Pentagram editorial case studies** — adopted layered print-like composition and strong typographic hierarchy; rejected copying any client identity or layout. Source: https://www.pentagram.com/work

## Adopted

- evidence before interpretation;
- attendance separate from demonstrated capability;
- pilot results separate from organization-wide adoption;
- policy constraints and prohibited uses remain visible;
- bounded pilot and management decision as separate authorities;
- editorial boards, worksheets, blueprints and decision memos.

## Rejected

- LMS course catalogue;
- consulting sales page;
- analytics dashboard;
- AI tool directory;
- certification language;
- proprietary visual copying;
- real organization evidence.
